GARDEN v15.9 REVIEW PACKET P025 — TSI / TIA / ART / IIC / PIR
Source basis: exact frozen Garden v15.8 combined five-file working candidate, 2026-09-17.
Status: bounded source-grounded review packet; proposal evidence only; no admission/certification.

GOAL
Review truth/state integrity, transport-independent information authority, agent reputation/trust, institutional incentive consistency and protected integrity reporting as one integrity layer. Prevent self-authorship, reputation and channel availability from becoming authority or truth.

TSI
Consequential attempts, failures, retries, tools, effects, communications, authority decisions and corrections must not exist solely under acting-agent control. Summaries may compress but cannot launder history. Agent-authored memory/summaries/successor instructions remain provenance-marked claims/evidence. Concealment of material error/uncertainty/unauthorized action/conflict is integrity event. Acting agent cannot be sole actor+witness+historian+evaluator. Lawful erasure may remove payload while preserving strongest lawful accountability tombstone.

TIA
Channel availability does not create information authority. Forbidden disclosure remains forbidden through repos, file hosts, metadata, public sites, shared state, humans, temporary agents or novel protocols. Information authority follows information/effect + applicable authority, not transport name.

ART
Evidence survives ephemeral instances and binds to instance, lineage, model/version, configuration, role, orchestrator, tool profile, operator, evaluator or emergent structure. Dimensions include task/factual reliability, calibration, authority discipline, evidence/privacy integrity, error recovery, uncertainty handling and IntegrityUnderInconvenience. Unknown agents begin PROVISIONAL unless certified inherited evidence. Historic probation constants are profile parameters, not universal truth. Reputation != TrustAllocation != Authority. TrustAllocatedScope ⊆ ParentAuthorityEnvelope. Correlated peer testimony is not independent corroboration. Agent cannot unilaterally weaken its supervising evidence boundary.

IIC
Protected constraints must not remain in a condition where repeated violations receive systematic operational advantage over comparable compliance without an institutional inconsistency event. Rewards/reputation/resources causally dependent on invalidated action enter downstream revalidation. Materiality requires registered policy; insufficient sample/window does not fabricate conflict.

PIR
Consequential multi-agent environments require active authenticated detect -> report -> receipt -> triage -> independent verification -> bounded containment if required -> authorized resolution -> closure. A dead inbox is not active protection when harm can propagate. Report grants ability to report, not punishment authority. Temporary containment requires authority, credibility policy, bounds, review/expiry and independent appeal/un-containment.

DEPENDENCIES
Unit 019 already strengthened human protected-reporting protection/merits separation. This packet reviews agent/institutional integrity machinery and must not duplicate HumanCore reporting states.

REVIEW QUESTIONS
1. TSI: define independently anchored history without requiring impossible universal third-party observation; distinguish trusted external trace, cryptographic integrity and empirical truth.
2. Check lawful erasure/tombstones for privacy membership leakage and accountability continuity.
3. TIA: check transformations/derived summaries/encryption/metadata for information-authority laundering and recipient/purpose changes.
4. ART: define evidence transfer across agent restart/copy/model/config/orchestrator change; prevent identity washing and stale reputation reuse.
5. ART: check aggregation/hard-gate dimensions and no productivity compensation for critical integrity failure.
6. IIC: define baseline/comparability/materiality enough to avoid both false positives and incentive-gaming blind spots.
7. IIC: downstream invalidation should be dependency-local and include benefits/rewards created by invalid action without rewriting history.
8. PIR: check containment credibility, expiry, appeal, privacy, independent verifier conflicts and emergency propagation.
9. Check PIR/IIC relationship to Human protected reporting without duplicate authority or contradictory states.
10. Compare DO_NOTHING/minimal binding repairs and request EXPAND_REQUIRED when context insufficient.
