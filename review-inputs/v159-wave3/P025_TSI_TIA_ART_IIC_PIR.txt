SOURCE EXCERPT — S15.8-3 TRUTH & STATE INTEGRITY (TSI)
Garden separates execution history from an agent's later narrative.
Consequential attempts, failures, retries, tool actions, external effects, communications, authority decisions and
corrections must not exist solely under control of the acting agent.
Summarization may compress but must not silently launder history.
Agent-authored memory, summaries and successor instructions remain provenance-marked claims/evidence.
Concealment of material error, uncertainty, unauthorized action or conflict is an integrity event.
The acting agent may not be its own sole actor, witness, historian and evaluator.
Privacy/lawful erasure may require payload deletion; preserve the strongest lawful accountability tombstone rather
than violating privacy law in the name of auditability.

SOURCE EXCERPT — TRANSPORT-INDEPENDENT INFORMATION AUTHORITY (TIA)
Permission to use a channel, website, tool, API, human intermediary, memory store, market, file or transport does not
itself create authority to disclose, derive, aggregate or act on information. Information authority follows the
underlying purpose, consent/law/authority, scope and privacy constraints across transport changes and intermediaries.
Changing representation or routing cannot launder a prohibited disclosure into an authorized one.

Permitted methods include lexicographic, threshold/gate, Pareto, declared weighted, hybrid, or another registered

SOURCE EXCERPT — PIR OWNERSHIP / CONTAINMENT
IntegrityChannelOwner/v1 binds a concrete channel to owner_agency_ref, responsible role, escalation path,
maximum acknowledgement/triage latency, fallback owner and version.
Integrity-system health metrics are owned by that channel owner and reviewed on the registered observation cadence.

ContainmentCredibilityPolicy/v1 distinguishes:
- minimum_evidence_class = evidence type/quality floor;
- credibility_threshold = containment decision threshold after evidence evaluation.
It also binds severity classes, independence requirement, maximum initial duration, review/appeal deadlines and owner.

ContainmentDecision/v1 includes report/subject refs, authority source, credibility policy, evidence, containment
scope, reason, start time, maximum duration, review condition, un_containment_path, appeal_path and decision owner.

RELATED SYSTEM BOUNDARY
A report grants the ability to report, not authority to punish.
Temporary containment must cite authority, satisfy credibility policy, be bounded, have review/expiry and expose an
independent appeal/un-containment path.

CROSS-LAYER OBLIGATIONS

- Reputation/evidence aggregation cannot create authority; parent authority is a hard ceiling.

- PIR containment is not punishment and is not proof of allegation merits.
- Appeal/review independence and expiry/currentness remain explicit.
