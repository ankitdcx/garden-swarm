CURRENT KNOWLEDGE SEMANTICS
Knowledge stores observations, evidence, claims, theories, interpretations, provenance and snapshots. Validation can change while immutable source/history remains inspectable. Contradictions can remain CONFLICTED/UNKNOWN/INCONCLUSIVE. Revalidation can be triggered by new evidence, changed assumptions/environment/dependency, contradiction, staleness or higher assurance demand.
KnowledgeSnapshot and EnvironmentCommitment bind uses to current epistemic/environment state.

WORLD EVIDENCE GRAPH — v15.8
Garden may maintain a provenance-rich graph separating:
1 raw evidence;
2 typed semantic world state;
3 purpose-limited agency profiles;
4 active retrieved context;
5 model weights/general learned structure.
Weight memory != evidentiary ledger. Copies do not multiply evidence weight. Minority evidence may not be destroyed because rare/unpopular. Private-device/private-record ingestion requires valid purpose + consent/law/authority/privacy and minimum necessary controls.
Technical rules: maintain lineage/dependence; high-consequence entity merge needs stronger evidence and reversible merge/split history; active retrieval returns smallest-sufficient relevant subgraph including provenance, contradictions, competing explanations, uncertainty and applicable authority/rights/policy; disputable/privacy/provenance-critical facts remain explicit rather than only weight memory.
Existing tests: repeated article does not multiply evidence; minority original remains visible against derivatives; private device without authority is blocked.

CURATION
Curation may deduplicate, normalize, correct, retract, annotate, compact and prioritize, but must preserve provenance, contradictions, retractions, lawful erasure and historical epistemic state. Curation cannot manufacture Evidence or erase material minority/counter evidence through popularity.

GSL-KR BOUNDARY
GSL-KR may implement current Knowledge storage/query semantics, but replaceability must remain possible through an owner-qualified Knowledge interface. Repository indexes/caches/embeddings are implementation projections and cannot become semantic truth sources.
