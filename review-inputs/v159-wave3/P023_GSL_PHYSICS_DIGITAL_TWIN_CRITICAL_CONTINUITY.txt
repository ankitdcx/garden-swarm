Arrival order is never silently treated as physical causal order. A timestamp without a declared clock domain,
synchronization uncertainty and provenance is insufficient for certified assimilation. Non-identifiable or
non-observable state remains UNKNOWN/DEGRADED rather than receiving false precision. A failed assimilation loop cannot
self-authorize a new fallback; its fallback must already be qualified for the exact physical scope.

GSL-PHYSICS v1.1 retains the v1.0 quantity/unit/frame/calibration/model/simulation/conservation foundation and adds the
temporal telemetry, out-of-order/jitter, causal-order, identifiability and event-loop contracts above.

PhysicalTwin is the owner-qualified GSL-PHYSICS specialization of DigitalTwin. It binds physical state/model identity,
units/frames, event-time frontier, last admitted telemetry, uncertainty/covariance, synchronization drift, staleness,
calibration/environment state, immutable assimilation history, model residuals, validity regime and fallback binding.
It is not a second unqualified DigitalTwin definition.

PhysicalDataAssimilationResult records admitted/rejected/quarantined sample identities, ordering/watermark decision,

The function pair preserves immutable raw telemetry and historical assimilation results. Latest arrival is never
silently equated with latest physical state. Late correction never rewrites past evidence in place; it creates a linked
successor state and invalidates affected dependents. A deterministic ordering algorithm is not safety evidence.

RELATED CPI BOUNDARIES

CRITICAL-CONTINUITY DEPENDENCY
The existing design separates authority revocation from physical shutdown. Critical continuity may require a bounded,
prequalified safe-control handoff when abrupt loss of control would itself be dangerous. A fallback/control path does
not gain authority from failure; it must carry independent legitimate authority/credentials, verified state transfer,
and unresolved-effect state. Suspect/revoked commands remain fenced or stale.

CROSS-LAYER QUESTIONS THAT MUST REMAIN DISTINCT
- PhysicalTwin model state != observation.
- Logical/causal ordering != a universal physical-clock accuracy guarantee.
- Telemetry admission != actuation authority.
- Deterministic filtering/order != safety proof.
- DigitalTwin/PhysicalTwin ownership must not duplicate the canonical DigitalTwin root.
- Controlled-safe-state/shutdown choice is domain/profile specific; no universal halt shortcut.
