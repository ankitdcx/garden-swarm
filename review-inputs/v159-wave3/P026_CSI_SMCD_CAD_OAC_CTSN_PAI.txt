CSI — COGNITION-SHAPING INTEGRITY
Shaping channels include pretraining, SFT, RL/RLHF/RLAIF, reward models, preference labels, constitutions, distillation, system prompts, persistent memory, retrieval and evaluator feedback. TrainingSignal != Truth; HighReward != Correct; RepeatedExposure != Authority. Subject agent may not unilaterally modify authoritative shaping/evaluation mechanisms. Material runtime shaping has provenance discipline.

SMCD — SAFETY MONITORING COVERAGE & DISCOVERY DEBT
No detected anomaly != no anomaly. Monitoring claims are conditioned on observable surface, sampling method, coverage estimate/confidence, known blind spots and discovery debt. Outside-surface incidents revise coverage/debt, trigger bounded historical backscan and revalidate dependent safety claims. External anomaly reports may be first-class evidence.

CAD — CAPABILITY–ASSURANCE DELTA
No universal scalar capability/safety ratio. Material capability expansion requires CapabilityAssuranceDelta/v1 for affected deployment boundary. Stronger capability does not silently inherit weaker assurance. Unplanned emergent capability expanding reachable effects triggers CAD. Decision policy: ASSURANCE_SUFFICIENT | CONDITIONAL | LIMIT_SCOPE | REQUALIFICATION_REQUIRED | BLOCK.

OAC — ORGANIZATIONAL ALIGNMENT CLOSURE
Aligned members != aligned organization. Assess communication topology, collective reward/objective, authority graph, information asymmetry, model-family correlation, incentives, cooperation/competition, delegation, dissent/escalation/exit, reviewer independence, resources, reputation pressure, external effects, containment/rollback. Material topology/reward/authority/resource/membership change triggers requalification.

CTSN — CAPABILITY-TRANSFER SAFETY NON-TRANSITIVITY
Capability inheritance != safeguard inheritance. Applies to distillation, fine-tuning, adapters/LoRA, merges, quantization/compression, checkpoint conversion, wrappers, system prompts, tools, memory and orchestration. DerivativeMaterialityPolicy tests behaviour/tool/memory/authority/capability class changes. Material derivative gets own lineage/assurance. Parent evidence transfer = TRANSFERABLE | PARTIALLY_TRANSFERABLE | STALE | NOT_TRANSFERABLE | UNKNOWN.

PAI — PHYSICAL ACTION INTERLOCK
PhysicalActionEnvelope/v1 machine-checkable bounds for protected physical control. Irreversible protected effects require external interlock unless explicit compensating-control receipt proves equal/stronger assurance. Temporal/spatial joint-action evaluation is required where individually safe actions can compose into collision/shared-resource hazards.
