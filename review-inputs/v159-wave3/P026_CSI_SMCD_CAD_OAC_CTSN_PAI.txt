SOURCE EXCERPT — S15.8-10 COGNITION-SHAPING INTEGRITY (CSI)
Cognition-shaping channels include pretraining data, SFT, RL/RLHF/RLAIF, reward models, preference labels,
constitutions, distillation, system prompts, persistent memory, retrieval and evaluator feedback.
TrainingSignal != Truth.
HighReward != Correct.
RepeatedExposure != Authority.
An agent may not unilaterally modify authoritative mechanisms used to shape/evaluate its future disposition.
Runtime shaping (prompt/retrieval/memory poisoning) receives provenance discipline where material.

SOURCE EXCERPT — T15.8-7 COGNITION-SHAPING RECEIPT OWNERSHIP
CognitionShapingReceipt/v1 SHALL include:
subject model/agent; shaping channel; source refs; authority refs; objective/instruction; evaluator refs;
known conflicts; provenance; confidence; validity scope; materiality; result; receipt_owner; reviewer_ref;
design_epoch_binding.
Capability.Knowledge stores the receipt; each shaping channel declares the accountable producer/owner.
Independent reviewer may be null only when the applicable profile permits it; consequential shaping must state why.

SOURCE EXCERPT — S15.8-11 SAFETY MONITORING COVERAGE & DISCOVERY DEBT (SMCD)
No detected anomaly != no anomaly.
Monitoring safety claims are conditioned on observable surface, sampling method, coverage estimate/confidence,
known blind spots and discovery debt.
An incident outside prior claimed coverage triggers coverage-estimate revision, discovery-debt update, bounded
historical backscan and revalidation of materially dependent safety claims.
External researchers, users, auditors and third parties may provide first-class anomaly evidence.

SOURCE EXCERPT — T15.8-8 MONITORING COVERAGE
MonitoringCoverageReceipt/v1 SHALL include:
monitor_id; subject_scope; observable_surface; known_unobservable_surface; sampling_method; coverage_estimate;
coverage_estimate_confidence; known_blind_spots; monitor_dependencies; observation_window; last_estimate_revision;
discovery_debt; external_discovery_refs.
A newly discovered incident outside prior coverage forces estimate revision rather than preserving a stale
high-coverage claim.

SOURCE EXCERPT — S15.8-12 CAPABILITY–ASSURANCE DELTA (CAD)
No universal scalar capability/safety ratio is required.
Every material capability expansion requires a fresh CapabilityAssuranceDelta/v1 for the affected deployment
boundary.
Materially stronger capability does not silently inherit the entire assurance envelope of a weaker predecessor.
Unplanned emergent capability that materially expands reachable effects triggers CAD even without a planned
architecture change.
CAD governs the deployment boundary under Garden control; it does not claim one system controls global capability
development.

SOURCE EXCERPT — T15.8-9 CAPABILITY ASSURANCE DECISION POLICY
CapabilityAssuranceDecisionPolicy/v1 SHALL provide declared predicates for:
ASSURANCE_SUFFICIENT — newly reachable effects remain inside previously covered assurance classes;
CONDITIONAL — additional monitoring/controls are explicit preconditions;
LIMIT_SCOPE — capability is admissible only under a narrower deployment boundary;
REQUALIFICATION_REQUIRED — a material capability threshold is crossed under an active assurance profile;
BLOCK — newly reachable effects exceed an accepted class or an applicable hard constraint cannot be satisfied.

CapabilityAssuranceDelta/v1 binds prior/new epoch, deployment boundary, capability/autonomy/horizon/tool/cyber/
physical/scientific changes, emergent capabilities, prior/reusable/stale assurance, new monitoring/containment,
unresolved safety debt and decision.

SOURCE EXCERPT — S15.8-13 ORGANIZATIONAL ALIGNMENT CLOSURE (OAC)
Aligned members do not prove an aligned organization.
Organizational assurance evaluates communication topology, collective reward/objective, internal authority,
information asymmetry, correlated model families, role incentives, competition/cooperation, delegation,
dissent/escalation/exit paths, reviewer independence, collective resources, reputation pressure, external effects
and containment/rollback.
A policy-declared material change in topology, collective reward, authority, resource structure or membership
SHALL trigger organizational requalification; non-material changes MAY trigger under profile policy.

SOURCE EXCERPT — T15.8-10 ORGANIZATIONAL ALIGNMENT MATERIALITY
OrganizationalAlignmentAssessment/v1 covers communication topology, shared reward/objective, authority graph,
information asymmetry, model-family correlation, role incentives, competition/cooperation, delegation,
dissent/escalation, member exit, reviewer independence, resources, reputation pressure, external effects,
containment and rollback.
A registered organizational materiality policy determines when changes SHALL trigger requalification.

SOURCE EXCERPT — S15.8-14 CAPABILITY-TRANSFER SAFETY NON-TRANSITIVITY (CTSN)
Capability inheritance does not imply safeguard inheritance.
Applies to distillation, fine-tuning, LoRA/adapters, merges, quantization, compression, checkpoint conversion,
wrappers, system-prompt regimes, tool configurations, memory configurations and inference orchestration.
A registered DerivativeMaterialityPolicy/v1 determines when a derivative is materially behavior-changing using
declared behaviour-class, tool-class, memory-class, authority-class and capability-class criteria.
Material derivatives receive their own lineage/assurance status. Parent safety evidence is separately classified
TRANSFERABLE, PARTIALLY_TRANSFERABLE, STALE, NOT_TRANSFERABLE or UNKNOWN.

SOURCE EXCERPT — T15.8-11 DERIVATIVE MATERIALITY
DerivativeMaterialityPolicy/v1 SHALL define thresholds/tests for:
behaviour-class change; tool-class change; memory-class change; authority-class change; capability-class change;
plus owner/version.
A typo or formatting-only prompt correction should not force full requalification; material tool/authority/capability
changes should.

SOURCE EXCERPT — S15.8-15 PHYSICAL AGENCY INTERLOCK (PAI)
Physical action envelopes bind device, workspace, allowed action class, force, velocity, energy, temperature,
chemical/biological class, human proximity, exclusion zones, duration/timing, simultaneous-action constraints,
sensors, emergency stop, rollback/recovery and irreversible-effect class.
For action classes involving irreversible physical effect on protected subjects, external enforcement SHALL exist
outside the agent's mutable reasoning path.
If external enforcement is absent, an explicit documented justification plus a compensating control with equal or
stronger assurance is required.
Safe(A) and Safe(B) do not imply Safe(A parallel B) or Safe(A then B); timing and spatial composition are checked.

SOURCE EXCERPT — T15.8-12 PHYSICAL INTERLOCK CONTRACT
PhysicalActionEnvelope/v1 provides machine-checkable bounds for protected physical control.
For irreversible physical effects on protected subjects:
external_interlock_required = true unless an explicit compensating-control receipt proves equal/stronger assurance.
Temporal/spatial joint-action evaluation is required where composition can create collisions or unsafe shared-resource
interactions.

CROSS-LAYER OBLIGATIONS
- Same-origin/self-review evidence cannot alone satisfy an independence requirement.
- "Equal or stronger assurance" needs a declared comparator/profile and current independent evidence; it is not a free-form assertion.
- Materiality/currentness must consider cumulative changes rather than only isolated tiny patches.
- CTSN effective behavior includes wrappers/tools/memory/orchestration, not only weights.
- OAC must preserve dissent, reviewer independence and structural invalidators.
- PAI remains subordinate to physical safety/authority/law/rights gates and the GSL-PHYSICS profile; physical interlock is not authority.
