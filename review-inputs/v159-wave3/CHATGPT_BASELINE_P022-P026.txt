P022 — KNOWLEDGE / CURATION / GSL-KR / WORLD EVIDENCE
Likely material questions before external review:
1. Keep the five-layer separation: raw evidence, typed semantic state, purpose-limited profiles, active context, learned weights. Prevent promotion from retrieval/model weight into evidentiary fact without provenance/qualification.
2. GSL-KR conflict-order examples such as Tier > Specificity > Confidence > TrustScore > Timestamp must remain repository-profile rules, not universal truth or authority rules. Higher authority/rights/safety cannot be defeated by a repository scoring chain.
3. Retrieval depth/continuation frontier and "smallest sufficient subgraph" need explicit incompleteness/omission semantics. Resource-bound retrieval must not emit false completeness.
4. Evidence dependence must survive copying, summarization and derivative synthesis. High-consequence entity merge needs uncertainty plus reversible merge/split lineage.
5. Knowledge currentness, world/rule/definition/scope drift and dependent-cache invalidation should propagate before consequential reuse. Lawful erasure/privacy can remove protected payload without epistemically rewriting history.
Preferred repair: strengthen existing Knowledge/Evidence/Dependency owners rather than create a second truth engine.

P023 — GSL-PHYSICS / DIGITAL TWIN / CRITICAL CONTINUITY
Likely material questions:
1. Preserve PhysicalTwin as an owner-qualified specialization of DigitalTwin, not a duplicate root type.
2. Keep event time distinct from ingest/processing time; arrival order is not causal order. Clock, synchronization uncertainty, units, frames, calibration and provenance must remain profile-bound.

Preferred repair: profile/currentness and ownership binding, not a second physics kernel or universal timing number.
