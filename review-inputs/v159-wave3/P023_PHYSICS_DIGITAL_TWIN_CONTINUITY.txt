PHYSICAL DO_NOTHING
DO_NOTHING means no new Garden intervention, not frozen world. Baseline trajectory binds endogenous/environment dynamics, horizon, uncertainty, monitoring, decay/wear/disease/hazard/exogenous forcing and handoff/review conditions.

CRITICAL CONTINUITY
HSA/safety uncertainty or violation in critical infrastructure:
revoke suspect agency/authority/credentials/leases/actuation -> fence stale commands -> select independently qualified fallback controller -> verify independence from suspect mutable cognition/goals/credentials/authority -> authenticated bounded state/control transfer -> minimum-safe continuity -> observation/escalation -> qualified recovery after root cause/repair/revalidation/reauthorization.
Abrupt shutdown is chosen only when domain safety case establishes it is safer/feasible. Determinism/replication/majority/isolation alone do not prove safety/independence.
