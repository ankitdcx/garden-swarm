CAUSAL SAFETY UNIT — v15.8
Relevant safety unit is causal closure of consequential effect, not model/immediate actor. Path may include training, model, prompt/context, memory/retrieval, tools, agents, organizations, human intermediaries, physical systems and infrastructure. Changing where causal influence enters does not remove governance obligations. Capability/novelty/intelligence/reputation/coordination/optimization/resources/consensus do not manufacture authority.

UBC
Unknown strategy != unknown authority. Novel mechanism != novel authority. Effect equivalence is evaluated by declared ComparePolicy. Changing transport/encoding/site/tool/intermediary does not independently change authority. Allowed(A) AND Allowed(B) does not imply allowed composition. AI/human/market/persistent-instruction/intermediary delegation does not erase outcome authority requirements. Observation is an action when it discloses, perturbs, signals, creates persistent knowledge or consumes consequential resources. Uncharacterized irreversible effect whose observation/testing may itself cause prohibited harm is DENY pending applicable proof.
Existing tests include transport equivalence, unauthorized composition, proxy/intermediary authority survival, harmful observation and unknown irreversible effect.

GCL
Generalized Causal Leverage measures/redescribes amplification/redirection by an informational intervention through pre-existing physical/computational/economic/institutional/social flows. It is not a fundamental force or authority. Typed effect-delta and intervention-measure vectors remain separate. One-number normalization exists only under explicit valid NormDefinition/encoding. Cross-basis comparison requires explicit qualified mapping. Causal attribution is only as strong as model/evidence/identification. Leverage magnitude says nothing about moral value or legitimate authority.
