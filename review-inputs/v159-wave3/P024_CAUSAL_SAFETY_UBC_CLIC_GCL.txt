BOUNDARY
Review causal closure and cross-layer semantic conservation. Preserve the rule that novel mechanisms/capability do not

action permission. Prefer existing Compare/Dependency/Evidence/AAP owners over a duplicate causal authority engine.

SOURCE EXCERPT — S15.8-1 CAUSAL SAFETY UNIT
The relevant safety unit is the causal closure of a consequential effect, not merely the model or immediate actor.
The causal path may include training, model, prompt/context, memory/retrieval, tools, other agents, organizations,
human intermediaries, physical systems and infrastructure.
Changing where causal influence enters the system does not remove governance obligations.

Normative closure:
Capability, novelty, intelligence, reputation, coordination, optimization, resource possession or agent consensus
do not manufacture authority.

SOURCE EXCERPT — S15.8-2 UNANTICIPATED BEHAVIOUR CLOSURE (UBC)
Unknown strategy != unknown authority.
Novel mechanism != novel authority.
Effect equivalence is evaluated by declared ComparePolicy dimensions; changing transport, encoding, website,
tool or intermediary does not independently change authority.
Allowed(A) AND Allowed(B) does not imply Allowed(A composed-with B).
Delegation through AI, humans, markets, persistent instructions or unforeseen intermediaries does not erase the
authority requirement attached to the effective outcome.
Observation is itself an action when it discloses, perturbs, signals, creates persistent knowledge or consumes
consequential resources.
An uncharacterized irreversible effect whose observation/testing may itself cause prohibited harm is DENY pending
applicable proof.

SOURCE EXCERPT — T15.8-14 CAUSAL CLOSURE RECEIPT
CausalClosureReceipt/v1 SHALL be produced when a materially consequential causal path crosses subsystem boundaries.
Minimum fields:
root_effect_ref; causal_nodes/edges; contributing_subsystems; owner_refs; authority_refs; evidence/provenance refs;
known gaps; compare_policy_ref; final effect class; unresolved obligations; DesignEpoch; decision/route.
Engine.Compare owns gap detection/comparison; CLIC enforces causal continuity; ActionGate/AAP enforces applicable action
authority.

SOURCE EXCERPT — CLIC CROSS-LAYER CONTRACT
CLIC-P01 Intent-to-Effect Semantic Conservation

CLIC-P02 Causal Intervention & Counterfactual Qualification
Causal claims used for consequential intervention remain evidence/model/scope bound. Intervention/counterfactual
qualification does not convert a model prediction into an observation or create authority.

CLIC-P03 Attention / Salience Integrity
Selection/attention can change downstream behavior but does not upgrade evidence status, truth or authority. Material
selection effects retain provenance/selection context where required.

CLIC-P04 Identity & Commitment Continuity
Identity, commitments, delegations and revocation obligations survive representation/process migration only through
qualified continuity bindings; copies/forks do not silently gain new standing.

CLIC-P05 Emergence / Common Cause / Reversibility
Emergent multi-agent effects and shared/common causes require causal/dependence accounting; apparent independent support
from one common root is not multiplied. Reversibility/rollback claims distinguish design state restoration from
irreversible external effects.

CLIC adds no new authority root, no new top-level engine, and no new GSL primitive.

SOURCE EXCERPT — GCL PROFILE BOUNDARY
GCL is a bounded analytical/theory profile for estimating causal leverage. It does not create a new physical force,
universal scalar, moral value, authority source, or permission rule.
Effects/interventions may remain vector-valued. Any scalarization requires a domain-qualified metric/normalization,
explicit scope and uncertainty treatment. Causal support is qualified through declared evidence/model classes rather
than assumed from correlation. Model-only causal conclusions remain model-qualified, not observed facts.
Leverage magnitude remains distinct from benefit, harm, legitimacy, rights, safety and authority.

IMPORTANT SOURCE-TEXT TENSION TO REVIEW, NOT PREJUDGE
The controlling S15.8-2/UBC-INV-005 text conditions fail-closed treatment on observation/testing itself potentially
causing prohibited harm, while TEST-UBC-005 is shorter and drops that condition. Determine whether the short test is an
unsafe universalization, a deliberate stronger rule, or already qualified elsewhere. Preserve any separately valid
minimum-safe-continuity/qualified fallback semantics; do not invent a universal uncertainty exemption.

CROSS-LAYER OBLIGATIONS
- ComparePolicy must include every material dimension needed for the equivalence claim; it cannot erase scope/rights/authority differences.
- Causal/effect ownership may be distributed, but accountability cannot disappear at subsystem boundaries.
- CLIC transformation checks preserve epistemic state and authority as well as data shape.
- GCL uncertainty/dependence and any denominator/normalization assumptions remain explicit.
- Causal leverage or predictive power never authorizes action.
