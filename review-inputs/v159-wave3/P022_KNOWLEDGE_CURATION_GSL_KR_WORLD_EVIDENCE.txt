SOURCE EXCERPT — USER U15.8-3 WORLD EVIDENCE / PRIVACY BOUNDARY
Garden may maintain a provenance-rich world evidence graph separating:
(1) raw evidence,
(2) typed semantic world state,
(3) purpose-limited agency profiles,
(4) active retrieved context,
(5) model weights/general learned structure.

Weight memory != evidentiary ledger.
Copies do not multiply evidence weight.
Minority evidence may not be destroyed merely because it is rare or unpopular.
Private-device or private-record ingestion requires valid purpose plus applicable consent/law/authority/privacy
and minimum-necessary controls. Technical access capability creates no permission.

RELATED REPUTATION BOUNDARY IMMEDIATELY ABOVE
Relative ranking requires a defined population, provenance, uncertainty and contestability.
Sparse evidence widens uncertainty rather than manufacturing extreme rank.
No demographic identity substitutes for behavior evidence.
No reputation score may silently become eligibility, policing, punishment, medical authority or reduction of
fundamental rights without separately valid law/policy, authority and rights review.

SOURCE EXCERPT — TECHNICAL T15.8-15 WORLD EVIDENCE GRAPH TECHNICAL RULES
Maintain evidence lineage/dependence so copies do not inflate support.
High-consequence entity merge requires stronger evidence and reversible merge/split history.
Active retrieval returns a smallest-sufficient relevant subgraph including provenance, contradictions, competing
explanations, uncertainty and applicable authority/rights/policy.
Current, disputable, privacy-sensitive or provenance-critical facts remain explicit when possible rather than being
treated as unchallengeable weight memory.

SOURCE EXCERPT — CURRENT GSL-KR PROFILE (THEORIES 8.2)
GSL-KR v1.3 — normative repository baseline: API Gateway → skeptical/default truth-maintenance/model solver →
append-only Hot/Warm/Cold/Archive ledger → optional governed action. Source trust may use Beta(α,β) profiles; conflict
policy may use Tier > Specificity > Confidence > TrustScore > Timestamp. A reference query-depth bound of 12 returns
INCOMPLETE plus a continuation frontier when reached; it is not a proof-completeness claim. These are repository
profiles, not universal truth rules.

GSL-AR v3.3 is an operational architecture baseline and GSL-R v1.0 is an exploratory/non-normative continuous-manifold
profile. Neither silently replaces GSL-KR; replacement requires ordinary versioned DesignBinding/migration gates.

SOURCE EXCERPT — CPI KNOWLEDGE RULES RETAINED IN THE SOURCE
CPI-017 — Qualified reuse only
Requirement: Reuse SHALL require current applicability, dependency, authority, and qualification bindings; UNKNOWN SHALL not be treated as APPLICABLE.

CPI-019 — No epistemic laundering
Requirement: Compression, repetition, retrieval rank, or migration SHALL NOT upgrade an assertion to established fact or erase material disagreement.

CPI-020 — Maintenance preserves material exceptions
Requirement: Retirement or abstraction SHALL preserve required exception coverage and lawful provenance; privacy deletion and retention conflicts SHALL have explicit dispositions.

CPI-021 — Typed epistemic outcomes
Requirement: Proof, likelihood, source trust, uncertainty, and preference SHALL retain distinct meanings; no undeclared scalar conversion may authorise action.

CPI-022 — Bounded uncertainty
Requirement: Resource exhaustion or inconsistency SHALL produce an explicit partial/unknown result rather than an invented proof or forced agreement.

CPI-023 — Semantic preservation boundary
Requirement: A bridge SHALL retain claim status, units, time, scope, and effects or explicitly report loss; graph or embedding similarity is not equivalence.

CPI-025 — Prediction is not observation
Requirement: Imagined states, synthetic rewards, and simulated successes SHALL remain labelled as model-generated and cannot independently validate real-world claims.

CPI-026 — Drift invalidates dependent assurances
Requirement: A material model, environment, or horizon change SHALL trigger re-evaluation of the policies and claims qualified using it.

CPI-027 — Grounding provenance
Requirement: Every consequential sensor-derived conclusion SHALL preserve measurement provenance and interpretation uncertainty.

CROSS-LAYER OBLIGATIONS
- Evidence copies/summaries sharing a causal root are not independent corroboration.
- An incomplete/resource-bounded retrieval must preserve its frontier/omissions rather than appear complete.
- Current-use claims must retain time/scope/applicability and dependency bindings.
- Privacy/lawful erasure may require payload removal; epistemic history must not be rewritten as if the evidence was refuted or never existed when a lawful non-identifying tombstone can be retained.
- Knowledge may inform decisions but creates no authority to act.
