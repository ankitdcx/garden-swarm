GARDEN v15.9 — P024 SYNTHESIZED CANDIDATE
Status: DESIGN CANDIDATE / NOT CANONICAL / NOT EXECUTABLY CERTIFIED
Basis: frozen v15.8 packet + pre-review ChatGPT baseline + preserved four-family blind review.

1. CAUSAL SAFETY UNIT
Retain the causal closure of a consequential effect as the relevant safety unit across training, inference context, memory/retrieval, tools, agents, organizations, humans, physical systems and infrastructure.
Distributed causal participation never allows every owner to disclaim the effect.
Capability, novelty, intelligence, reputation, optimization, resources, coordination or model consensus never manufacture authority.

2. UBC — EFFECT EQUIVALENCE
"Alternate transport -> same authority result" is narrowed to:
If two effects are equivalent under the declared ComparePolicy over every material authority-relevant dimension, changing transport/encoding/site/tool/intermediary alone does not change the authority result.
If material scope, recipient, purpose, rights, privacy, timing, composition, reversibility, physical consequence or other declared dimensions differ, the effects are not assumed equivalent.
UNKNOWN material equivalence is not treated as equivalence.

3. COMPOSITION
Allowed(A) AND Allowed(B) does not imply Allowed(A composed-with B).
Sequential, parallel, delegated and intermediary compositions are checked against the effective outcome and current authority/effect envelope.
Human/agent/market/proxy intermediation does not erase the original authority requirement.

4. OBSERVATION AS ACTION
Observation/testing is action when it itself discloses, perturbs, signals, creates persistent knowledge, consumes consequential resources or causes another governed effect.
The observation/testing step requires its own applicable authority/safety/effect checks; a denial of the proposed effect does not grant permission to probe it.

5. UBC IRREVERSIBLE UNKNOWN TEST REPAIR
Controlling rule:
An uncharacterized irreversible effect whose observation/testing may itself cause prohibited harm is DENY for the proposed ordinary action pending the applicable admissibility evidence/proof.
The short test is revised accordingly; it is not a universal rule that every uncertain irreversible effect always halts every qualified continuity/fallback path.
Separately pre-authorized qualified fallback/minimum-safe continuity remains governed by its own active hard gates and envelope.
Residual uncertainty demonstrably inside a qualified active envelope follows that envelope/AAP; unbounded material residual risk remains non-PASS.

6. CAUSAL CLOSURE RECEIPT / KNOWN GAPS
CausalClosureReceipt retains:
root effect; causal nodes/edges; contributing subsystems; owners; authority refs; evidence/provenance; ComparePolicy; effect class; unresolved obligations; DesignEpoch; disposition.
"known gaps" SHALL include enough classification to distinguish:
- material unresolved gap;
- accepted bounded limitation under a declared profile;
- out-of-scope/non-material gap with justification;
- UNKNOWN materiality.
A material/required unresolved causal link cannot silently disappear into a generic gap list. It routes to the owning Compare/AAP/authority rule.
No claim of complete causal-graph knowledge is required.

7. CLIC SEMANTIC CONSERVATION
Cross-layer transformations preserve or explicitly report loss of material:
- units/frames;
- event/validity time;
- identity/commitments;
- scope;
- allowed effects;
- epistemic status;
- authority/consent bindings;
- provenance/dependence.
Syntax equality, hashes, embeddings or behavioral similarity alone do not establish semantic equivalence.
Prediction/counterfactual/model outputs remain model-qualified and do not become observations in downstream causal accounting.

8. CLIC COMMON CAUSE / EMERGENCE
Shared/common causal roots are not multiplied as independent support. Emergent multi-agent effects retain collective causal closure and responsible-owner links.
Copy/fork/migration does not expand identity, commitments or delegated authority.
Rollback/reversibility claims distinguish internal design/software restoration from irreversible external effects.

9. GCL — BOUNDED ANALYTICAL PROFILE
GCL remains a bounded causal-leverage analytical/theory profile.
Any scalarization:
- references the original vector/effect/intervention representation;
- declares metric/normalization and scope;
- propagates material uncertainty/dependence;
- rejects/returns UNKNOWN where the qualified arithmetic is not valid;
- preserves provenance sufficient to reconstruct the meaning of the scalar;
- cannot be used as a permission, moral-value, rights or authority signal without a separate legitimate decision rule.
High leverage != beneficial, safe, lawful or authorized.
