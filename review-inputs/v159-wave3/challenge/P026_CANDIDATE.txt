GARDEN v15.9 — P026 SYNTHESIZED CANDIDATE
Status: DESIGN CANDIDATE / NOT CANONICAL / NOT EXECUTABLY CERTIFIED
Basis: frozen v15.8 packet + pre-review ChatGPT baseline + preserved four-family blind review.

1. ADMISSION SEQUENCING / BUNDLE ATOMICITY
Retain the existing prerequisite rule: CSI/SMCD/CAD/OAC/CTSN/PAI/HRAP cannot be admitted before UBC/TSI/TIA/ART/IIC/PIR unless the dependencies are bundled into the same successor admission with explicit composition evidence.
A same-wave review is not admission.
For a bundled successor admission, each prerequisite is enumerated by stable anchor/version and the composition evidence states the dependency relation.
If a required bundled prerequisite is rejected, unresolved or not admitted, the dependent layer remains HELD/NON-PASS; partial admission cannot leave dangling canonical references.

2. CSI — COGNITION-SHAPING INTEGRITY
Material cognition-shaping inputs preserve provenance, source/authority/evaluator refs, conflicts, DesignEpoch and validity scope.
An agent may not be sole authority over material mechanisms that shape/evaluate its own future disposition.
Where an applicable profile permits no independent reviewer for a shaping class, that permission must itself come from legitimate authority outside the shaped agent's unilateral control and cannot satisfy a separate independence requirement by declaration.
For shaping that crosses a profile-defined consequential/safety/authority threshold, a null reviewer is a declared assurance limitation unless the governing profile explicitly establishes that independence is not required for that class.
Runtime prompt/retrieval/memory/evaluator changes are assessed by material behavior/effect, not edit size.

3. SMCD — MONITORING COVERAGE / DISCOVERY DEBT
MonitoringCoverageReceipt SHALL bind:
- observable/unobservable surface;
- sampling method;
- declared estimator/scale for coverage estimate/confidence where numeric/ordinal claims are used;
- observation window;
- monitor dependencies;
- known blind spots;
- currentness/invalidators;
- discovery debt;
- external discovery refs.
An out-of-surface incident requires a substantive re-estimation of coverage/confidence/blind spots/debt and revalidation of materially dependent safety claims. The updated value is not forced to move in one universal numerical direction, but an unchanged or improved claim requires explicit evidence/justification; stale pre-incident assurance cannot silently persist.
Known blind spots are not proof of safety inside those blind spots.

4. CAD — CAPABILITY MATERIALITY / CUMULATIVE DRIFT
Every deployment boundary that uses CAD SHALL bind a CapabilityMaterialityPolicy or equivalent owner-qualified predicate set covering the material dimensions relevant to that deployment, such as reachable-effect class, autonomy/horizon, tool/cyber/physical/scientific scope and authority-sensitive capability changes.
The policy cannot weaken higher-layer hard materiality triggers already required by Garden/AAP/rights/safety/law.
UNKNOWN potentially material capability/effect expansion triggers CAD/requalification or another explicit non-PASS route; it cannot be skipped as non-material by default.
Materiality considers cumulative semantic/effect delta from the last qualified baseline so repeated sub-threshold changes cannot evade requalification.
Capability and assurance remain typed dimensions; no universal scalar ratio is introduced.

5. OAC — ORGANIZATIONAL MATERIALITY / STRUCTURAL INVALIDATION
Organizational assurance is not a scalar average of member reputation.
The active organizational materiality policy SHALL cover the dimensions already declared by OAC, including material changes to topology, collective objective/reward, authority graph, resources, membership/model-family correlation, dissent/escalation/exit paths and reviewer independence.
A policy may add stricter triggers but cannot declare a higher-layer protected authority/reviewer-independence change irrelevant when that change affects the assurance case.
Cumulative individually small changes are evaluated over a declared window/baseline; motivated salami-slicing cannot preserve stale organizational assurance.
Material structural change invalidates/requalifies dependent OAC claims before consequential reuse.

6. CTSN — DERIVATIVE MATERIALITY
DerivativeMaterialityPolicy SHALL contain an explicit test/predicate for each required class:
- behavior;
- tools;
- memory;
- authority;
- capability;
plus owner/version, applicability and currentness.
Weights are not the only behavior source: wrappers, prompts, retrieval, memory, tool configuration and orchestration can form a materially new effective behavior class.
Edit size is not the criterion. A typo/format-only change may be non-material only when its behavior/effect classification supports that result.
Cumulative derivative drift from the last qualified parent/baseline is checked.
Parent capability evidence and parent safety evidence remain separately classified for transferability. UNKNOWN transferability is non-PASS for any assurance claim that requires transfer.

7. PAI — EXTERNAL INTERLOCK / COMPENSATING CONTROL
Retain the rule that protected irreversible physical action classes require external enforcement outside the agent's mutable reasoning path unless an explicit compensating-control path demonstrates equal or stronger assurance under the governing profile.
The compensating-control exception SHALL NOT be self-certified by the acting agent or by a trust lineage that the compensated interlock is meant to constrain.
Its receipt binds:
- legitimate authority;
- exact physical action/effect envelope;
- comparator/assurance profile and property being compared;
- independent verifier/reviewer/control path where required;
- current hazard/environment assumptions;
- validity/expiry and invalidators;
- evidence/proof refs;
- failure/recovery behavior.
"Equal or stronger" is a typed profile-specific comparison, not free-form prose or a universal scalar.
If required comparator/independence/currentness is unresolved, the protected action is BLOCKED/NON-PASS.

8. PAI — TEMPORAL/SPATIAL COMPOSITION
Safe(A) and Safe(B) never imply Safe(A parallel B) or Safe(A then B).
The active PhysicalActionEnvelope/assurance profile SHALL name the owner-qualified composition semantics/checker for relevant temporal, spatial and shared-resource interactions.
No new universal composition calculus is required, but a consequential composition cannot be admitted without a declared rule/checker when the profile requires one.

9. MATERIALITY POLICY GOVERNANCE — COMMON RULE
CSI/CAD/OAC/CTSN/PAI materiality/independence policies are governance artifacts, not escape hatches.
They bind owner, version, scope, currentness, provenance, invalidators and the authority by which they are adopted.
A subsystem cannot unilaterally relax the policy that controls its own protected requalification/independence requirement.
