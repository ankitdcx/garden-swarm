1. TSI — INDEPENDENT HISTORY WITHOUT IMMORTAL PAYLOAD
Consequential attempts, failures, retries, tool actions, external effects, communications, authority decisions and corrections SHALL NOT exist solely under control of the acting agent.
The independence requirement may be satisfied by a protected audit/receipt/history path that the acting agent cannot unilaterally rewrite, suppress or certify for itself; it does not require a second human observer for every event.
Agent-authored summaries remain provenance-marked interpretations, not independent observation.

Where privacy/lawful erasure applies, payload may be deleted/quarantined. Preserve only the strongest lawful minimum accountability tombstone/structural record permitted for the governing purpose.
A tombstone binds, where lawful and necessary:
- record/event identity or non-identifying surrogate;
- class/type of event;
- erasure/retention disposition and authority basis;
- minimal timing/provenance linkage;
- affected dependency/revalidation refs;
without retaining protected content that must be erased.
Erasure is not relabeled as refutation and does not create a false narrative that a historical event never occurred when lawful structural history can remain.

2. TIA — INFORMATION AUTHORITY AT POINT OF USE
Channel/tool/API/file/device/network/market/human-intermediary access does not create disclosure, derivation, aggregation or action authority.
For consequential information use, the existing authority/access/privacy/action receipt path SHALL resolve and record the current:
- purpose;
- principal/subject where applicable;
- consent/law/authority basis;
- scope/data class;
- permitted operation and recipient/effect envelope;
- expiry/invalidators/currentness;
- provenance.
No new universal authority root is required; use the existing authority/consent/privacy owners and record their refs.
If the underlying purpose/authority is ambiguous, stale, revoked or conflicting, the information use is non-PASS until a legitimate resolution/fallback rule applies.
Transport or representation changes cannot launder a prohibited information use.

3. TRUST ALLOCATION — MACHINE-READABLE CONDITIONS
TrustAllocationDecision remains bounded by ParentAuthorityEnvelope:
  TrustAllocatedScope <= current ParentAuthorityEnvelope.
ALLOW/CONDITIONAL/DENY conditions that affect runtime admission SHALL be machine-resolvable or explicitly bound to an owner-qualified human/legal review route; free-text alone cannot satisfy a machine-enforced condition.
Point-of-use revalidation is required after parent-envelope revocation/supersession/narrowing. Until revalidation completes, the old decision is STALE/non-PASS for newly consequential use.
Risk class and verification level SHALL resolve to registered owner-qualified profiles rather than ungrounded labels.

4. REPUTATION AGGREGATION
ReputationAggregationPolicy must state dimensions, hard gates, aggregation method, missing/stale/correlated evidence rules, owner/version and applicability.
Shared-root/correlated evidence SHALL NOT be multiplied as independent support.
Missing or materially UNKNOWN evidence cannot be turned into compliant/PASS merely because a policy declares a permissive default where a hard requirement needs evidence.
A registered aggregation method cannot override parent authority, rights, law, privacy or hard safety constraints.

5. EPOCH INDEPENDENCE
Recording reviewer identity is not enough to establish independence.
The active profile SHALL define the material conflict/affiliation/control relationships that disqualify independence and the evidence required to establish it.
Evidence producer/reputation owner cannot be the sole final classifier where independence is required.
A superseding NOT_INDEPENDENT or invalidated classification removes future predictive contribution and flags materially dependent current decisions for revalidation; provenance history is not rewritten.

6. IIC MATERIALITY / DECISION RULE
IncentiveMaterialityPolicy retains separate systematic-advantage and consequence-magnitude dimensions plus comparability, observation window, minimum sample, correlation rule, protected classes, owner/version.
MATERIAL_CONFLICT or an equivalent strong institutional conclusion is permitted only when the active policy's required evidence, comparability, sample/window and threshold predicates are satisfied.
If sample, comparability, independence or materiality is insufficient/unknown, return INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE / UNKNOWN / NON_MATERIAL_WITHIN_SCOPE as appropriate rather than forcing a conflict finding.
Observed adverse outcomes alone do not prove incentive conflict.

7. PIR / PROTECTED REPORTING OWNER REUSE
Reuse the P019 candidate semantics for reporter protection and allegation merits rather than creating a second lifecycle:
- reporting protection status is separate from allegation merits;
- unsupported/mistaken/withdrawn does not by itself establish bad faith;
- independent adverse action requires a separately supported legitimate basis and retaliation review where applicable;
- reporting creates no punishment authority or blanket immunity.
PIR owns the protected integrity channel/containment mechanics; Human-Effect/Reporting semantics remain one consistent cross-layer rule.

8. CONTAINMENT — MINIMUM NECESSARY, BOUNDED, NON-PERPETUAL
ContainmentCredibilityPolicy and ContainmentDecision SHALL bind evidence floor, credibility threshold, severity class, authority, scope, independence, expiry/review, appeal/un-containment path and owner.
Containment scope/effect must be minimum necessary for the current legitimate safety/integrity purpose under the active profile.
A report/allegation is not itself containment authority or proof of merits.
Serial renewal cannot create indefinite containment by reset. Renewal requires fresh current authority/evidence and obeys an owner-qualified cumulative duration/review bound or equivalent hard termination/escalation rule. If a profile permits extended containment, that basis remains explicit and contestable/reviewable as required.

9. IIC↔PIR LINK
Where protected reporting alleges a material institutional incentive conflict, the report may route to IIC assessment while retaining PIR/reporting protections.
The route does not convert the allegation into an IIC finding. IIC outcome and reporting protection remain separately typed.
