GARDEN v15.9 — P022 SYNTHESIZED CANDIDATE
Status: DESIGN CANDIDATE / NOT CANONICAL / NOT EXECUTABLY CERTIFIED
Basis: frozen v15.8 packet + pre-review ChatGPT baseline + preserved four-family blind review.

1. KNOWLEDGE LAYER SEPARATION
Retain the five distinct layers:
(1) raw evidence;
(2) typed semantic world state;
(3) purpose-limited agency/reputation profiles;
(4) active retrieved context;
(5) model weights/general learned structure.
No bridge, compression, retrieval rank, solver output or migration may silently collapse these layers.
Weight memory, model inference and synthetic material are not independent observations.

2. EVIDENCE DEPENDENCE / CAUSAL ROOTS
Evidence aggregation SHALL use the existing provenance/dependence owner to retain stable evidence identity and source-root/dependence references sufficient to detect copies, summaries, citations, derived transforms and later-discovered common roots.
Shared-root material is not independent corroboration and is de-duplicated for independence-sensitive support.
If dependence is materially UNKNOWN, the result retains that uncertainty; it is not counted as independent merely because a root could not be resolved.
No new universal evidence-voting engine is created.

3. ACTIVE RETRIEVAL SUFFICIENCY / FRONTIER
"Smallest-sufficient relevant subgraph" means the smallest bounded subgraph that satisfies the active RetrievalProfile for the stated purpose and includes, when known and material within the search frontier:
- provenance/source identity;
- material contradictors and competing explanations;
- uncertainty/status;
- applicable authority/rights/policy constraints;
- required exceptions/dependencies.
The profile declares its stopping/resource rule and omission/frontier semantics.
If a depth/resource/context bound is reached before the profile's sufficiency conditions are established, retrieval returns INCOMPLETE plus a continuation/omission frontier.
Any materially derived conclusion SHALL carry the INCOMPLETE/frontier binding until a later qualified completion supersedes it. Stripping this marker is epistemic laundering.

4. CONFLICT POLICY IS NOT UNIVERSAL TRUTH PRECEDENCE
A registered GSL-KR conflict ordering such as Tier > Specificity > Confidence > TrustScore > Timestamp applies only after currentness, scope/applicability, type compatibility and authority boundaries required by that profile have been checked.
It cannot:
- turn a stale/inapplicable claim into a current fact;
- collapse proof, observation, probability, preference or policy into one scalar status;
- hide material contradiction;
- override rights/authority/safety/law.
Where materially incompatible epistemic states or unresolved currentness/scope conflicts remain, preserve CONFLICT/UNKNOWN/CONTESTED rather than manufacturing one winner.

5. TRUST PROFILE BINDING
Beta(alpha,beta) or another source-trust profile is a repository profile, not a universal truth operator.
Its update/decay/prior semantics, evidence dependencies, validity/currentness and invalidators SHALL be declared by the active profile.
Lawful erasure, discovered dependence, source invalidation or material definition/scope drift triggers recomputation or STALE/REVALIDATION_REQUIRED for dependent trust/assessment artifacts.

6. APPEND-ONLY HISTORY + LAWFUL ERASURE
Append-only epistemic history does not require immortal private payload.
Where privacy/lawful erasure requires deletion:
- delete/quarantine the protected payload according to the owning privacy process;
- retain only the strongest lawful minimum accountability/provenance tombstone or structural record;
- never relabel erasure as empirical refutation or pretend the historical event never occurred when lawful history can be retained;
- tombstoned payload does not contribute current evidential support unless a separately lawful retained representation remains usable;
- materially dependent current assessments are invalidated/recomputed as required.
Merge/split reversal is additive: append correction/split/supersession records rather than destructively rewriting prior history.

7. MODEL/SYNTHETIC OUTPUT LABEL CONSERVATION
Solver/model-generated/synthetic outputs preserve their epistemic type and provenance through ledger storage, retrieval and migration.
A model-generated record cannot be reclassified as an independent real-world observation by storage, repetition, summarization or graph placement.
If a bridge cannot preserve claim status, time, units, scope or effect semantics, it reports semantic loss under the existing CPI/Bridge rules.

8. KNOWLEDGE DOES NOT AUTHORIZE ACTION
The optional governed-action stage SHALL resolve a separately valid current authority/purpose/consent/law/policy basis before consequential use.
A trust score, retrieval result, reputation value, evidence count or model conclusion can inform an authorized decision but cannot itself create permission, punishment, eligibility, disclosure or other rights-affecting authority.

9. ENTITY MERGE/SPLIT
High-consequence entity merges require profile-qualified evidence and uncertainty. Merge/split lineage is reversible at the semantic-view level through append-only successor/correction records; historical evidence is not rewritten in place.
