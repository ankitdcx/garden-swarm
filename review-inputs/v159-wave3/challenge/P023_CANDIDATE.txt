Status: DESIGN CANDIDATE / NOT CANONICAL / NOT EXECUTABLY CERTIFIED
Basis: frozen v15.8 packet + pre-review ChatGPT baseline + preserved four-family blind review.

1. PHYSICAL BOUNDARY / RECEIPT
Retain GSL-PHYSICS v1.1 ingress/egress semantics. For consequential physical boundary crossings, PhysicalIngressEgressReceipt binds at minimum:
- source/target physical boundary refs;
- typed material/energy/measurement flow classes;
- units/frames/transforms;
- measurement/calibration/environment context where applicable;
- uncertainty/error bounds;
- conservation/accounting result and any residual;
- source provenance;
- applicable profile/version;
- disposition and unresolved obligations.
A receipt records the evaluated property; it is not by itself permission to actuate or external certification.

2. TIMING / CAUSAL ORDER
Event/observation time remains distinct from ingest/processing time. Arrival order is never silently causal order.
TelemetryTemporalBinding SHALL bind the clock domain and synchronization/uncertainty needed by the active profile.
Where the requested assimilation semantics require causal/event-time ordering, at least one profile-qualified sequencing/causal anchor SHALL be available. If the required ordering cannot be established, return ORDER_UNKNOWN / BUFFER / QUARANTINE / DEFER / UNKNOWN according to the profile rather than inventing order.
Logical/causal ordering never proves a universal physical-clock accuracy bound.

3. DEFER / BUFFER / WATERMARK
The authoritative admission domain SHALL preserve a non-success state for measurements not yet admissible. Temporal delay may use BUFFER_UNTIL_WATERMARK; non-temporal pending conditions may use DEFERRED or an owner-qualified equivalent.
Reorder windows/watermarks are profile-bounded and declare:
- finite/bounded resource/time behavior where required;
- overflow/expiry disposition;
- late-data correction rule;
- liveness/backpressure behavior.
An unbounded buffer cannot masquerade as successful safe admission.

4. UNKNOWN / NON_IDENTIFIABLE / STALE / BLOCKED
UNKNOWN, NON_IDENTIFIABLE, STALE and BLOCKED are non-PASS for any hard requirement that needs resolved current physical state.
They cannot by themselves authorize a fallback or physical effect.
If uncertainty/covariance/residuals cross the active profile's admissibility bound, the owner-qualified policy selects DEGRADED / BLOCK / DEFER / prequalified fallback / controlled-safe-state as applicable. No universal numerical threshold is introduced.

5. PHYSICALTWIN SPECIALIZATION
PhysicalTwin remains the owner-qualified GSL-PHYSICS specialization of the canonical DigitalTwin identity.
A conforming registry/schema SHALL resolve the PhysicalTwin specialization to the canonical DigitalTwin owner/identity and state the added physical fields/constraints. It SHALL NOT register a duplicate unqualified DigitalTwin root.
If that root binding is unresolved, specialization conformance remains UNRESOLVED rather than inventing a second twin type.

6. MODEL STATE != OBSERVATION
PhysicalTwin state, prediction, filter output and simulation remain model-qualified state. They do not become independent observation merely because assimilation is deterministic or repeatedly successful.
Historical raw telemetry and assimilation dispositions preserve provenance. Late correction appends successor state/correction links and invalidates materially affected dependents rather than rewriting source evidence in place.

7. PREQUALIFIED FALLBACK / CRITICAL CONTINUITY
Fallback/control continuity does not gain authority from failure.
A DataAssimilationProfile/PhysicalTwin fallback binding SHALL identify:
- exact qualified physical scope/effect envelope;
- legitimate authority/credential basis;
- safety/assurance profile;
- independent control/verifier path where independence is required;
- validity/expiry and invalidators;
- transferred state requirements;
- unresolved-effect/state obligations;
- recovery/termination rule.
At use time, currentness and scope are revalidated according to the active contract.
A failed/suspect controller cannot self-authorize or solely certify its successor.
Abrupt shutdown is not universal; use the domain-qualified minimum-risk continuity/safe-state rule.

8. "CERTIFIED" WORDING
Timestamp/clock completeness is a prerequisite for any profile that requires it, but does not itself create certification.
Use "qualified assimilation under the active profile" unless an actual certification owner/process is explicitly referenced.

9. RETENTION / COMPACTION
"Immutable assimilation history" means prior admitted source/result lineage is not silently rewritten. It does not require unbounded immortal payload.
Profile-qualified lawful compaction/tiering/erasure may retain hashes/tombstones/summary state where allowed, provided material provenance, correction lineage and unresolved obligations remain auditable and privacy/law are preserved.
