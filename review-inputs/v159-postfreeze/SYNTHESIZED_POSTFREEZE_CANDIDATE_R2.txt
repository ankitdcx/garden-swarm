FINAL R2 SEMANTICS

R2-A — OPERATIONAL RESILIENCE / CIAH REFINEMENTS
A1. Saturation and overload SHALL resolve through existing typed runtime/AAP dispositions. No new saturation SchemaID or priority token is created. If a required rights/safety/authority/privacy/law gate cannot be satisfied under load, the affected work is BLOCKED/non-PASS or explicitly deferred with the gate preserved; queue pressure, volume, urgency or scarcity cannot make a required gate optional.
A2. Downstream saturation/backpressure that can affect correctness, completion or timing SHALL be explicit upstream state. Silent loss and unbounded queue growth are prohibited. The current Runtime/Transition owner determines the exact registered state vocabulary; this overlay does not mint a parallel state algebra.
A3. Recovery/failover revalidation explicitly includes recovered cache, in-memory state, authority/evidence/dependency bindings and uncertain external effects before ordinary restoration.
A4. Handoff/failover closure is not a separate owner. It reuses B3 Process/Execution/CPI acknowledgement semantics. A control switch without resolved state/effect identity, current ownership/authority and applicable acknowledgement remains INCOMPLETE/OUTCOME_UNKNOWN; it is not success.
A5. Monitor/alarm output creates no authority. Uncertain/false alarms remain typed and effect-bounded. Rights-affecting or irreversible protective action still requires the current applicable authority/safety profile; a noisy detector cannot self-authorize escalation.
A6. Material hazard analysis includes action, suppression/non-action, fallback and shutdown consequences. No universal numerical threshold is created by this requirement.
A7. Near-miss/precursor evidence may trigger NEEDS_REVALIDATION, research or bounded learning, but is not silently promoted to incident-grade truth, liability, guilt or authority.
A8. Cumulative-change anti-fragmentation remains the existing DesignEpoch owner; end-to-end human-effect coverage remains Human-Effect Closure. No duplicate CIAH owner is added.

R2-B — UAAC / DSDIH DISPOSITION
B1. UAAC is NOT admitted as a new subsystem. B2. Unresolved material adversarial channel/strategy coverage at the required assurance level is scoped UNKNOWN/INCOMPLETE and non-PASS for that assurance claim. “Bounded open-world” never lowers the required assurance level or turns UNKNOWN into PASS; exhaustive proof is not falsely claimed.
B3. Process/Execution/CPI is the single owner for dispatch/commit/effect/acknowledgement semantics. Crash or loss after external effect but before acknowledgement is OUTCOME_UNKNOWN and blind replay is forbidden absent existing qualified fencing/idempotency/reconciliation semantics.
B4. Cache, sidecar, proxy, adapter, queue or intermediary insertion cannot weaken applicable authority/rights/privacy/safety/law gates merely because it relays, caches or transforms work.

R2-D — MACHINE PROCESSING / PRIVACY / JUSTICE
D1. Universal everyone/all-the-time inspection of private devices/messages is explicitly REJECTED. Technical possibility, model confidence, security claims or social benefit do not create a general observation/search/surveillance authority.
D2. Machine-only computation on private content is itself a privacy/human-effect event even if no human views the content. It requires independently valid purpose plus applicable authority/consent/law/rights/privacy, minimization, retention limits and current evidence; absence of human viewing waives none of these.
D3. Computation/access, retention, human inspection, disclosure, evidentiary use, investigative threshold, adjudicative finding and guilt/liability are distinct stages. Every protected transition requires its own current legal/authority basis where applicable; no stage automatically authorizes or proves the next.
D4. Scoped privacy-preserving/on-device analysis may be admissible only under the same independently valid purpose/authority/consent/law/rights/privacy/minimization constraints. Where the operation is rights-affecting or evidentiary, applicable auditability and contestability are mandatory. Technical capability or model confidence never creates observation rights.
D5. Correlated suspicion remains epistemically distinct from jurisdiction-specific investigative thresholds, admissible evidence, finding of fact and guilt/liability. Garden does not invent one universal probable-cause or evidentiary threshold.
D6. Missing, stale or conflicting purpose, authority, jurisdiction, minimization, model-validity, auditability or other required evidence yields non-PASS for the protected operation.
D7. Existing Justice, TSI/TIA, Human-Effect Closure, Privacy and Authority owners remain controlling; no parallel police power, justice engine or surveillance subsystem is created.
