U15.8-1 — HUMAN-EFFECT CLOSURE
Any AI-originated action, omission, communication, optimization, adaptation, delegation, model update, tool use,
collective behavior or downstream causal effect that can materially affect one or more humans remains subject to
applicable constitutional, rights, consent, privacy, authority, safety, law/policy, evidence, provenance,
contestability, explanation and remedy requirements.

This remains true whether the effect is direct/indirect, immediate/delayed, intended/incidental, physical,
informational, economic, social, cognitive, institutional or environmental, produced by one agent or many,
visible or invisible, or understandable or not understandable to current humans.

UNKNOWN_HUMAN_EFFECT != NO_HUMAN_EFFECT.
Capability, intelligence, optimization quality, hidden representation, prediction accuracy, coordination,
reputation or majority agreement do not create sovereignty or permission.
