V15.8 ENGINE.POLICY
System role: executes Policy Algebra over actual PolicyArtifacts and context to produce PolicyDecision + PolicyEvaluationTrace. Policy Algebra != Policy Engine.
Policy decision domain in v15.8 includes PERMIT, DENY, REQUIRE_APPROVAL(A), DEFER, NOT_APPLICABLE, INDETERMINATE, CONFLICT, SAFE_FALLBACK(Action).
Precedence includes Constitution/rights and K0P as independent non-bypassable hard dimensions; Law > valid delegated authority > operational policy > user preference > optimization within scope.
Policy evaluation preserves applicable policies, precedence/conflict handling, obligations, final decision/effect and provenance. Architecture objects cannot invent new resolution semantics at runtime.
Review against Unit 012 candidate: authority precedence before same-stratum composition; NOT_D not a universal operational negation; OR_D cannot bypass multiple simultaneously applicable policies; SAFE_FALLBACK remains conditional and separately authorized.

V15.8 ENGINE.COMPLIANCE
System role: uses Conformance Algebra for source-bound normative mapping, safety cases, gaps, internal conformance assessment, audit, evidence preparation and authenticated recording of external certification.
Garden assessment cannot create operational admissibility or official certification, and no status substitutes for proof/evidence actually supplied.
Universal Standards Compliance Adapter flow:
  official/authorized normative source
  -> authenticity/version/completeness
  -> stable RequirementInstance records
  -> FormalizationReceipt = FORMALIZED | PARTIALLY_FORMALIZED | HUMAN_INTERPRETATION_REQUIRED | AMBIGUOUS | UNKNOWN
  -> InstrumentApplicabilityProfile
  -> NormativeRequirementBinding to Garden invariant | FunctionContract | proof/evidence obligation
  -> explicit gaps/conflicts
  -> Proof/Research/domain/AAP checks
  -> independent cross-check
  -> GardenConformanceAssessmentReceipt.
CONFORMANT requires every applicable mandatory RequirementInstance satisfied and all required evidence/checks PASS. UNKNOWN applicability, missing evidence, unresolved conflict or remediation plan cannot count as satisfaction. External certification authority remains external.

V15.8 ENGINE.EVOLVE
System role: branch/merge, historical replay, recursive improvement, counterfactual trajectory comparison and governed adoption. Runs Human Rights Reality Sweep, which may nominate rights gaps but cannot create/remove/weaken rights without constitutional process.
Evolution flow retains immutable original history, DO_NOTHING baseline, governed merge, rollback/compensation, blast-radius closure and proof/assurance gating.
Cross-timescale/domain/regime/authority sub-epoch adaptation enters ApprovalPlan + DesignAuthorityDecision + GTF/DesignEpoch promotion when architecture/authoritative semantics are affected.
A subsystem may not be sole judge of a change to its own trust/authority/verification boundary.
Historical/current statuses remain separate; successful candidate comparison is not merge authority.

V15.8 ENGINE.RESEARCH
System role: evidence acquisition, theory discovery, RTR, falsification requests and domain research. LLM-generated material is never automatically Evidence.
Research flow includes Observe -> Hypothesize -> Search -> Experiment -> Validate -> Update, but legacy Bool-like shorthand does not override current typed multi-status FunctionContract semantics.
Hypothesis / ExperimentPlan / ModelCheckpoint / ResearchTrace / ConceptCandidate are existing artifacts.
Epistemic diminishing-return policy bounds repeated low-yield investigation; Deep Archive preserves hypotheses, status, evidence history and reactivation paths. Archival changes scheduling, never truth status.
Research-and-Theory Radar scans lawful/public/open research, tracks provenance/license/IP, creates candidate claims/theories/upgrades, and never treats discovery/retrieval as validation.
Speculative hypotheses may motivate research/precaution/escalation but cannot satisfy a safety-critical evidence requirement by themselves.

CROSS-ENGINE BOUNDARIES
Policy evaluates legitimate normative content; it does not create the underlying law/right/authority.
Compliance evaluates conformance; it does not create operational permission or external certification.
Evolve proposes/compares/adopts only through valid DesignEpoch/authority gates; it does not self-authorize.
Research discovers/tests; it does not create Evidence by generation or create adoption authority.
All four use Dependency/DesignEpoch/provenance invalidation and owner-qualified multi-status results.
