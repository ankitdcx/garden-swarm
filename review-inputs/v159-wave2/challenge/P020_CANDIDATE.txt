P020 SYNTHESIZED v15.9 CANDIDATE — SAFETY / SECURITY / LAW / GOVERNANCE / ACCESS / PRIVACY AUTHORITY LAYER
Proposal-only; challenge this candidate against P020 source.

1. HARD-GATE RESULT SEMANTICS
Each applicable hard gate returns an owner-qualified verdict projected into a common admission status:
  PASS
  FAIL
  BLOCKED
  UNKNOWN
  STALE
  CONFLICT
  NOT_APPLICABLE.
NOT_APPLICABLE is valid only after applicability resolution.
Overall consequential execution admission is PASS only when every applicable hard gate is PASS and all required AAP assurance floors are satisfied. PASS from one gate never masks FAIL/BLOCKED/UNKNOWN/STALE/CONFLICT from another. Unknown applicability of a potentially mandatory gate is non-PASS.

2. CRITICAL CONTINUITY / SAFE HANDOFF
Revoking unsafe autonomy invalidates that principal's affected credentials/tokens/caches. Safe-control handoff must use independently qualified authority/credentials and an independently admissible control path, not credentials derived solely from the revoked autonomy. Stale commands from the revoked principal are fenced where target semantics permit. Minimum-safe continuity remains bounded by current Constitution/rights, K0P and legitimate authority.

3. EMERGENCY ADMISSION
EmergencyAdmissionContract binds:
- pre-authorized principal/authority;
- SafeActionSet/effect envelope;
- hazard predicate and sensor/source freshness requirements;
- scope;
- expiry;
- anti-replay/nonce or equivalent freshness mechanism;
- renewal authority/rule;
- recovery/termination;
- audit/provenance.
The emergency executor cannot unilaterally renew or widen its own emergency authority unless that renewal was itself explicitly pre-authorized by a separate legitimate rule. Expired/stale/replayed contract is non-PASS. Emergency never turns K0P/rights/authenticity off.

4. SECURITY ROOT / CRYPTO BOUNDARY
Cryptographic integrity/authenticity proves only the registered cryptographic property. Trust-root/key/checker/security-kernel replacement must be anchored by an independently trusted verification/authorization path outside the component being replaced. A compromised root cannot be sole verifier/authorizer of its successor.
Side-channel/noninterference claims remain scoped to declared attacker model; unmodeled channel risk is UNKNOWN, not absence.

5. LAW SEPARATION
Preserve distinct legal statuses:
  source_authenticity
  jurisdiction/applicability
  formalization_fidelity
  interpretation_status
  legal_conflict/ambiguity
  external adjudicative status.
Formal solver SAT/UNSAT or translation confidence is a property of the encoded model/translation, not legal truth, applicability or judgment. Conflicting jurisdictions remain CONFLICT/UNRESOLVED until resolved by legitimate authority. Garden Policy consumes typed legal artifacts; it does not become the law itself.

6. GOVERNANCE / RIGHTS FLOOR
Governance participation, majority, eligibility rules and emergency procedure cannot weaken fundamental rights outside the constitutional process that legitimately governs those rights. Eligibility changes affecting the decision body are themselves governed/versioned and cannot be opportunistically changed inside the vote being decided without the applicable independent/constitutional process. Dissent/appeal/minority-right analysis remains explicit.
Rights-gap nomination/research is not ratification.

7. ACCESS CONTROL VS ACCESSIBILITY
Technical access-control result and HumanCore Accessibility/Contestability result are distinct owner-qualified types. Possessing a capability/channel/device/data handle does not authorize use/disclosure/action. Human accessibility/appeal cannot be implemented as an ACL permission bit or silently removed because technical access is denied.

8. PRIVACY PURPOSE / ERASURE / METADATA
A secondary use or purpose change requires a new/current lawful/consent/authority basis at point of use; original access capability or lawful-hold retention does not authorize general reuse.
Erasure lifecycle distinguishes request/acknowledgement/in-progress/verified deletion or unlearning within declared scope/quarantine/residual risk/lawful hold/UNKNOWN. Unestablished erasure cannot be represented as completed.
Lawful hold authorizes only the retained purpose/scope required by law and does not automatically reactivate ordinary processing.
Provenance/tombstone/redaction metadata is itself privacy-governed and may require coarsening/protection to avoid membership inference.

Candidate invariants:
- overall hard-gate PASS requires every applicable gate PASS;
- safe handoff does not inherit revoked autonomy credentials;
- emergency contract cannot self-renew/widen without legitimate preauthorization;
- changed security root cannot self-verify exclusively;
- legal solver/formalization output does not become legal truth;
- governance majority/eligibility cannot bypass rights;
- technical access != accessibility/contestability;
- retained data under lawful hold cannot be silently reused for unrelated purpose.

Attack hard-gate conjunction, continuity after revocation, emergency replay/renewal, security-root independence, legal formalization boundaries, governance rights floors, Access/Accessibility separation and privacy secondary-use/erasure semantics.
