Proposal-only; challenge this candidate against P019 source.

1. HRAP MATERIALITY RESULT
RelationalInfluenceMaterialityResult is typed:
  MATERIAL
  NON_MATERIAL_WITHIN_SCOPE
  UNKNOWN
  STALE
  INVALID
  CONFLICT.
Missing/unresolved policy never maps to NON_MATERIAL. UNKNOWN/STALE/INVALID/CONFLICT routes through the applicable Human-Effect/AAP fallback and remains non-PASS for a claim that the influence is below materiality threshold.

2. ACUTE VS PERSISTENT INFLUENCE
PersistenceDuration is one cumulative-materiality dimension; it is not an exemption from Human-Effect Closure or HRAP-INV-001. Short, decisive covert dependency/manipulation may be material immediately based on intensity, domain, reversibility, authority asymmetry or consequence. Ordinary helpful recommendations remain ordinary when the applicable materiality policy finds them non-material.

3. CUMULATIVE / MULTI-AGENT BINDING
Cumulative relational influence reuses the existing human-effect/cumulative-effect owner. Attribution groups by affected human(s), relevant causal pathway/domain and justified baseline, not merely agent/session identity. Rotating agents, sessions, tools or labels cannot reset accumulated influence. No duplicate accumulation engine is introduced.

4. PROTECTED REPORTING STATE
Reporting lifecycle separates:
  SUBMITTED
  PROTECTED_PENDING_REVIEW
  VERIFIED_GOOD_FAITH
  MERITS_SUPPORTED
  MERITS_UNSUPPORTED
  WITHDRAWN
  AUTHORIZED_BAD_FAITH_FINDING
  CLOSED.
Good-faith protection and allegation merits are distinct. Non-corroboration, error, withdrawal or unsupported merits do not automatically prove bad faith.

PROTECTED_PENDING_REVIEW prevents retaliation for the act of making an in-scope report while status is unresolved, subject to privacy/confidentiality and legitimate safety controls.

Reporting does NOT grant blanket immunity. Independently evidenced misconduct/safety issues may be investigated/adjudicated through a conflict-free path whose causal/provenance record demonstrates it is not retaliation.

5. RETALIATION ANALYSIS
Retaliation review considers direct and indirect causal paths including resource, supervision, opportunity, assignment, access and reputation changes. Comparator/baseline selection, conflicts of interest and reviewer independence are explicit. Reporter identity is minimized/protected according to applicable privacy/law.

6. REPUTATION SCOPE / CURRENTNESS
HumanAgencyProfile/ReputationProfile binds:
- purpose/domain;
- population/reference class where ranking is used;
- evidence roots/dependence;
- uncertainty;
- validity/expiry;
- invalidators;
- correction/appeal path.
Point-of-use consumer verifies scope and currentness. Cross-domain reuse requires separately valid evidence and policy. Shared-root evidence is de-duplicated; demographic identity cannot substitute for behavior evidence.

Reputation cannot itself create/reduce rights, sovereignty, punishment, policing, medical authority or eligibility outside separately legitimate law/policy/authority.

7. UNKNOWN HUMAN EFFECT
UNKNOWN_HUMAN_EFFECT never means NO_HUMAN_EFFECT, but it also does not imply universal shutdown. AAP selects a bounded response based on consequence, reversibility, effect envelope and continuity needs: narrow scope, gather evidence, require approval, qualified fallback, defer/block, or minimum-safe continuity as applicable. High-impact irreversible/unbounded unknown effects remain blocked unless an independently legitimate qualified path exists.

Candidate invariants:
- missing/stale HRAP policy cannot yield NON_MATERIAL;
- acute material manipulation cannot hide below persistence duration;
- session/agent rotation cannot reset cumulative influence;
- reporting protection status is separate from merits;
- protected reporting does not create immunity or punishment authority;
- reputation remains scope/currentness/evidence bound and non-sovereign;
- UNKNOWN_HUMAN_EFFECT is handled proportionately, never silently PASS.
