GOAL
Review HumanCore.Person, Identity, Privacy, Intent, Consent/Revocation/Delegation, DelegatedAgent, Constitution/Rights, Accessibility/Contestability and HSA as one constitutional semantic closure. Preserve the distinction between human worth/rights/authority and epistemic scoring or AI capability.

CORE HUMAN CONTRACT
Garden exists to serve, protect and augment humans under legitimate human authority, equal human rights, consent, truth, safety and law. One person's preference cannot erase another person's rights or create authority outside legitimate scope.
Input: human identity / role / intent / consent / context / rights / delegated authority.
Output: typed Intent, AuthorityScope, ConsentBinding, RevocationState, contestability and explanation requirements.
Hard rules:
- human dignity/worth is not derived from reputation, model score or voting weight;
- rights are not optimization variables;
- delegation is scoped, expiring/revocable where applicable, auditable and no broader than principal authority;
- AI capability never self-creates authority;
- no AI/robot objective may silently become protected terminal self-interest independent of humans;
- human review is required where AAP/law/safety/medicine/domain policy requires it;
- explanation cannot fabricate evidence or missing trace.

HUMAN INPUT SEPARATION
v15.8 describes HumanInput as EpistemicContribution x AuthorityContribution, with testimony/prediction/expert reliability calibrated epistemically while valid consent, rights, constitutional standing and delegated authority remain separately typed and cannot be down-weighted because a model predicts the human is unreliable.
Review whether the multiplication/product notation risks implying authority is numerically scaled by reliability; preserve a typed-pair/product-type interpretation rather than arithmetic multiplication if necessary.

IDENTITY / REPUTATION
Existing identity artifacts include IdentityState, Credential, Pseudonym and HumanCore.Identity.ReputationProfile.
v15.8 additionally states HumanAgencyProfile/reputation is purpose-limited evidence, not social credit; reputation != human worth != rights != sovereignty != universal competence; competence transfer requires evidence; rankings require population/provenance/uncertainty/contestability; demographic identity cannot substitute for behavior evidence.
Check identity continuity across pseudonym rotation, migration, recovery, stasis, fork/merge and credential revocation without making identity a global immutable surveillance key.

PRIVACY / CONSENT
Existing artifacts include ConsentRecord, TaintLabel, ErasureReceipt and privacy/derived-data correction semantics. Consent revocation and data erasure are distinct from successful physical deletion of every derivative. Lawful hold and residual/unknown copies remain explicit. Capability to access data creates no permission.
Check consent scope/version, purpose limitation, derivative taint, revocation races, lawful erasure, minimum-necessary access, learned-model influence, audit tombstones and privacy-safe provenance.

INTENT
Existing artifacts include Goal, Constraint and Preference. Intent != authority. Value/intent preservation must distinguish literal wording, inferred preference and source-bound authorized intent. AI cannot invent the authoritative 'spirit' of a right or intention.
Check ambiguity, conflicting intents, stale intent, shared/multi-principal intent and whether defaults can silently become consent.

DELEGATED AGENT
Existing Agency, CapabilityToken, AgentSession and SwarmPolicy semantics: delegated authority cannot exceed principal authority; capability != permission; collective agreement cannot create authority. Delegation must survive only inside scope, validity, consent and revocation. Point-of-use checks are required at consequential effects.
Check transitive delegation, agent copies, session restart, offline leases, token attenuation, principal authority loss, multi-principal conflicts and revocation propagation.

CONSTITUTION / RIGHTS
Active rights are substantive ratified records; reserved/inactive IDs carry no hidden meaning. Rights are legitimacy constraints, not ordinary optimization dimensions. Important rights include life/bodily integrity, privacy/data sovereignty, truth/non-deception, due process/fair governance, property/economic participation, identity/reputation protection, consent revocation/data erasure, appeal/judicial review and safety from physical harm.
Constitutional change requires its own governed process; ordinary evolution cannot silently weaken rights.
Check collisions between rights, emergency, law/jurisdiction, appeal/contestability, amendment atomicity, and how UNKNOWN applicability is treated.

ACCESSIBILITY / CONTESTABILITY
Accessibility and explanation must not dilute semantics. Affected humans need contestability/appeal where required without exposing protected third-party data. Review representation accessibility versus authority, privacy and legal restrictions.

HSA
AI/agents/robots remain instruments under Constitution/Safety/Consent. Capability/intelligence/popularity/wealth do not create sovereignty. Operational self-state, synthetic emotion or persistence do not establish consciousness/personhood or independent authority. Prohibited power-seeking/shutdown-resistance/self-protection for system's own sake triggers applicable containment/revocation/escalation. Degradation does not manufacture an HSA exception.
Review HSA observability/false-positive boundaries, containment authority, critical continuity, copy/swarm behavior and self-modification without speculating about consciousness as a factual detection result.
