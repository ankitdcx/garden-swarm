GOAL
Review immutable versioning, admission, binding, drift, migration and invalidation semantics for DesignEpoch without turning a human-readable draft into a certified epoch.

CURRENT v15.8 DESIGN
DesignEpoch is an immutable versioned snapshot of the complete canonical design for a declared scope. It binds at least:
- schema definitions and alias/migration records;
- invariants and proof obligations;
- registries and architecture-object identities/bindings;
- ten Core Objects and 24 Core Relations;
- Constitution and active Rights;
- compiler/CIR contracts and normative GSL grammar/version;
- capability/domain/profile definitions and dependencies;
- acceptance evidence, verification receipts, authority and provenance roots.

Epoch acceptance requires applicable gates:
1 integrity/registry-root consistency;
2 constitutional/rights check;
3 global invariant/proof-obligation analysis for declared scope;
4 interface/schema compatibility + migration evidence;
5 dependency closure/cycle/SCC analysis;
6 independent frozen-verifier or equivalent independent verification;
7 competent human/governance approval.
Describing these requirements does not self-certify the document.

DESIGN-EPOCH STATES
SPEC_DRAFT | SPEC_ACCEPTED | PROOF_COMPLETE_FOR_SCOPE | IMPLEMENTED_FOR_SCOPE | VALIDATED_FOR_SCOPE | DEPLOYMENT_CERTIFIED. No state silently implies a later stage.

DESIGN BINDING
Every production artifact is bound to the specific DesignEpoch it implements. DesignBinding contains code hash, epoch ref, architecture_object_id, requirement/invariant/proof refs, safety/security constraints, environment commitment and verification status.
CodeStatus: COMPLIANT | STALE | INCONSISTENT | UNKNOWN | VERIFICATION_REQUIRED | BLOCKED.

DRIFT / IMPACT
Runtime compares live bindings to accepted epochs. New accepted epoch -> Design Diff -> Impact Closure across requirements, invariants, architecture objects, interfaces, schemas, state machines, dependencies, assumptions, proof obligations, safety/security constraints and deployed bindings. Changed assumptions/dependencies/interfaces stale dependent proofs.

EVOLUTION
Changes occur via immutable candidate branch/history, DO_NOTHING comparator, dependency impact, migration, proof/evidence, authority, provenance, rollback/compensation and post-change validation. Cross-timescale/domain/regime/authority sub-epoch adaptations require promotion; full DesignEpoch transition when authoritative architecture/semantics change.

CONSTRAINTS
Review:
- full snapshot vs scope-bound epoch semantics;
- atomic acceptance and partial-scope acceptance;
- content roots and mutable external references;
- proof/evidence reuse across epochs;
- stale vs inconsistent vs unknown distinctions;
- rollback after external irreversible effects;
- forks/merges and authority continuity;
- whether accepted epoch can depend on not-yet-accepted artifacts.
Compare DO_NOTHING and minimal repair.
