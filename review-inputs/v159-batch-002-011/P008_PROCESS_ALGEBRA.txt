GOAL
Review Process Algebra as Garden's central execution composition semantics. Strengthen operator contracts without duplicating Runtime, AAP, Transition or domain logic.

ALGEBRA ADMISSION RULE
An Algebra must define reusable typed operands, operators, composition/closure laws, identity/error/unknown behavior and interaction rules. Undeclared algebraic laws cannot be assumed.

CURRENT PROCESS OPERATORS

DO_NOTHING
Processes remain bounded by authority, invariants, effects, resources, failure/recovery and TerminationContract.

PROCESS ARTIFACTS
ProcessPlan = typed graph before executable admission.
ExecutableProcessPlan = admitted specialization with resolved versions, budgets, guards and receipts.
ProcessInstance = live/version-bound execution state.
ProcessCapabilityContract = architecture-object interface consumed by AAP.

PARALLEL
Requires ParallelCompositionContract declaring isolation, shared state, join condition, cancellation, partial-failure handling, compensation/rollback, timeout, ordering/finality and deadlock/livelock/fairness obligations. Failed child cannot be silently ignored because another succeeded. Parallel composition is not universally commutative at effect level.

RECONSTRUCT
Emits ReconstructionReceipt binding source records, signatures/attestations, membership epoch, environment, uncertainty and equivalence result. Reconstruction never recreates authority from untrusted history.

DO_NOTHING
Represented as DoNothingProcess with baseline, expected cost/risk of inaction, validity interval, monitoring and next-review condition. For dynamic physical/biological systems, DO_NOTHING means no new intervention by evaluated decision maker, not frozen world dynamics.

RETRY / EFFECT BOUNDARY
v15.8 engineering hardening says physical effects of uncertain completion must be reconciled before retrying; duplicate delivery/concurrent workers require effect identity/idempotency semantics where supported.

CONTINUOUS SERVICES
Bounded-recursion termination applies to recursive jobs. Long-running services instead use productivity/liveness/non-Zeno/fairness, bounded reaction and stoppability contracts.

CONSTRAINTS
Review:
- COMPOSE vs SEQUENCE ambiguity;
- operator input/output/result closure;
- retry after uncertain or irreversible effect;
- rollback vs compensation semantics;
- cancellation propagation and partial commit;
- branch condition UNKNOWN;
- parallel interference/shared authority;
- recursion vs long-lived service;
- DO_NOTHING baseline semantics;
- reconstruction equivalence and authority non-recreation.
Compare DO_NOTHING and minimal repair.
