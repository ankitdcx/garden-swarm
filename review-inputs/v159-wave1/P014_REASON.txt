GARDEN v15.9 REVIEW PACKET P014 — ENGINE.REASON / REASONING STRATEGY PORTFOLIO
Source basis: exact Garden v15.8 combined five-file working candidate, 2026-09-17.
Status: bounded review extract; proposal evidence only; no admission/certification.

GOAL
Review Engine.Reason as Garden's universal reasoning engine, including tiering, strategy selection/composition, abstraction, preimage/backward reasoning, claim-validation routing, resource bounds and reasoning-performance evidence. Keep Reason separate from Proof, Evidence, Search, Compare and Authorization.

CURRENT CORE
Engine.Reason selects/composes reasoning strategies.
Reasoning != Proof != Evidence != Authorization.
Current modes include ABSTRACTION and backward preimage/reachability analysis for necessary-condition/root-cause/WHY_NOT reasoning.
A typed ClaimValidationPlan routes promotable/consequential results to required formal, empirical, test, legal/authority and human-review paths.

CURRENT TIERS
Historical R0 transition sanity was moved by Unit 001 to a separate non-Reason SanityGate owned primarily by Engine.Orchestrate.
Engine.Reason modes are therefore:
  R1 — Local Reasoning: small local candidate set; cheap.
  R2 — Contextual Reasoning: knowledge, dependencies, constraints, multiple alternatives; moderate.
  R3 — Deep Reasoning: theory selection/composition, simulation, mathematical analysis, adversarial comparison and broader search.
  R4 — Critical Reasoning: maximum bounded assurance appropriate to scope, independent challenge and required governance/human escalation.
AAP selects the active tier. Reason tier is an assurance depth, not a fixed compute multiplier and does not automatically imply identical Proof/Safety tier.

REASONING STRATEGY PORTFOLIO CANDIDATE
ReasoningStrategyPortfolio contains candidate strategies with metadata such as strategy kind (LLM, planning, simulation, formal), performance history, resource footprint and assurance compatibility.
ReasoningStrategyPerformanceRecord is evidence-bound to strategy outcome in a context: accuracy/quality, latency, resource use, uncertainty and provenance.
REG-REASON-PORTFOLIO-001 binds strategy classes to admissible contexts, metrics and assurance floors.
Candidate FunctionContracts:
  assemble_reasoning_portfolio(problem_signature, context, assurance_profile)
  select_reasoning_strategy(portfolio, context, assurance_profile)
  record_reasoning_performance(strategy_ref, outcome, context)
Invariant intent:
- selection uses Compare and preserves hard constraints;
- performance records are provenance/dependency bound;
- no strategy bypasses Proof/Safety/Authority;
- composition obeys resource/termination bounds;
- selection emits a trace/receipt.

PREEMPTION / RESOURCE CONTEXT
AAP may consume ReasoningPreemptionContract and ProofResourceBudget where applicable. Reasoning is bounded; timeout/resource exhaustion is an explicit non-PASS result, not success.

CLAIM / EPISTEMIC BOUNDARY
Reason may generate deductions, hypotheses, candidate causal explanations, plans, abstractions, counterfactuals and validation plans. These outputs do not become Evidence, Proof or authorized action merely because reasoning generated them.
Common-sense/default reasoning is defeasible and cannot outrank proof, authenticated law, empirical evidence or hard safety constraints.

EXISTING CROSS-CUTTING USERS
- CLIC QualifyCausalClaim and AllocateAttention are owned by Engine.Reason.
- CSR, causal world-model reasoning, theory selection and mathematical reasoning use Engine.Reason with Knowledge/Compare/Research/Proof as appropriate.
- Unit 001 causal level semantics constrain causal conclusions.
- Unit 010 AAP determines assurance floors.
- Unit 011 FunctionContract governs executable reason operations.

REVIEW QUESTIONS
1. Does R1–R4 tiering have clear semantic obligations rather than vague 'think harder' labels? Define minimum differences without hardcoding one implementation.
2. Define ProblemSignature and strategy-selection criteria enough to prevent circular 'best strategy according to itself'.
3. Distinguish strategy performance evidence from benchmark leakage/self-grading and context shift.
4. Check mixed strategy composition (LLM + planner + solver + simulator + formal methods) for termination, state/provenance and conflicting conclusions.
5. Check abstraction/refinement and backward/preimage reasoning for soundness bounds and overclaim prevention.
6. Define output/result algebra for REASON: solved, partial, inconclusive, unknown, contradiction, resource/time bound, model out-of-scope, validation-required.
7. Check causal reasoning against C0..C4 and identification obligations.
8. Check self-improvement/evolution of reasoning strategy portfolio: no self-certification of protected evaluator changes.
9. Compare DO_NOTHING/simplification and request EXPAND_REQUIRED for materially missing context.
