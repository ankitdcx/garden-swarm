GARDEN v15.9 REVIEW PACKET P013 — ENGINE.COMPARE / GSL-COMPARE
Source basis: exact Garden v15.8 combined five-file working candidate, 2026-09-17.
Status: bounded review extract; proposal evidence only; no admission/certification.

GOAL
Review Engine.Compare / GSL-COMPARE as Garden's universal comparison mechanism. Strengthen epistemic separation, hard-gate handling, policy scoping, comparator evolution and machine-resolvable output without duplicating Reason, Proof, Research or Policy.

CURRENT SEMANTICS
Engine.Compare compares external/internal proposals against canonical Garden using bidirectional gap analysis and multi-dimensional constraint-aware comparison. Current comparison also models evidence dependence, regime instability/tail uncertainty and comparator self-evolution assurance.

Three stages:
1. Hybrid semantic + symbolic retrieval.
2. Recursive closure + bidirectional gap analysis.
3. Multi-dimensional comparative judgment with Proof/evidence/constraint checks.

Comparison normalization extracts proposal claims/actions/relations/policies, assumptions, ambiguities, context and provenance into a typed semantic model.

Candidate retrieval includes mandatory symbolic sets before optional ranking:
  ExactSymbolic
  UNION MandatoryDependencies
  UNION ApplicablePolicies
  UNION RelevantInvariants
  UNION TopKSemantic
  UNION HistoricalMatches
Similarity ranking may prioritize optional candidates but cannot prune mandatory constitutional/safety/dependency/policy edges.

Stage-2 labels:
  AGREEMENT
  CONTRADICTION
  OMISSION
  AMBIGUITY
  NOVELTY
A semantic atom retains parent context, qualifiers, scope, negation, temporality, authority and dependencies.

Comparison dimensions may include:
  evidence quality
  formal consistency
  empirical validation
  explanatory power
  invariant satisfaction
  safety
  authority/constraint feasibility
  scope/applicability
  simplicity
  coverage
  predictive success
  cost
  reversibility
  historical performance
Each dimension carries value/ordering, uncertainty, confidence and evidence basis.

HARD-GATE RULE
Constitution/rights, K0P, required authority, mandatory invariants and minimum evidence/assurance are feasibility constraints. Pareto comparison occurs among feasible candidates. Cost/speed cannot compensate for a violated hard gate.

EPISTEMIC / ADOPTION SEPARATION
  epistemic_result = which claim/model is better supported?
  design_quality_result = which architecture is better on applicable design dimensions?
  constraint_status = what hard constraints/authority apply?
  adoption_recommendation = what should Garden do?
Canonical status itself does not establish truth. Strongest valid evidence appropriate to claim type dominates canonical status on factual support. Mathematical claims require appropriate proof; empirical claims appropriate evidence; normative/legal adoption also requires legitimate authority.

Comparison preserves dominance / incomparability / inconclusive / unknown outcomes and routes adoption, retention, merge, research or escalation separately from epistemic support.
Comparison output preserves agreements, contradictions, proposal/Garden gaps, dimension results, hard-constraint status, recommendation, unresolved questions, explanation trace and provenance.

Counterfactual option:
  World A = current Garden
  World B = Garden + proposal
Replay representative historical/synthetic operations and compare correctness, safety, latency, resources, failures, proof burden and explainability where applicable.

COMPARE POLICY
ComparePolicy declares:
  comparison dimensions and metric family;
  domain/unit/frame bindings;
  tolerance or equivalence/non-inferiority region;
  uncertainty/dependence treatment;
  materiality threshold;
  hard bounds that cannot be compensated;
  escalation/research behavior;
  validity scope and DesignEpoch.
No universal physical or semantic percentage is implied.

REVIEW QUESTIONS
1. Define exact output/result algebra: dominance, equivalence, incomparability, inconclusive, unknown, conflict and partial comparability.
2. Ensure epistemic_result, design_quality_result, constraint_status and adoption_recommendation cannot be accidentally conflated.
3. Check dimension selection/materiality/scalarization for hidden value judgments or compensation across hard constraints.
4. Check comparison under missing/stale evidence, dependence, tail/regime uncertainty and conflicting models.
5. Check counterfactual replay for model leakage, hindsight bias, non-identifiability and false evidence promotion.
6. Check comparator evolution/self-calibration: the comparator cannot be sole judge of a change to its own protected evaluation boundary.
7. Check ComparePolicy versioning/freshness and cross-domain metric incompatibility.
8. Prefer simple partial-order / typed-result semantics over universal scalar scoring.
9. Compare DO_NOTHING and request EXPAND_REQUIRED for materially missing source anchors.
