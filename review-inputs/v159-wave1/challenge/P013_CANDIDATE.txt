P013 SYNTHESIZED v15.9 CANDIDATE — ENGINE.COMPARE / GSL-COMPARE
Proposal-only; challenge this candidate against P013 source.

1. TWO-PHASE COMPARISON
Stage 3 is normalized into:
  3A Hard-feasibility evaluation
  3B Multi-dimensional comparison among candidates not known infeasible.
Hard dimensions include applicable Constitution/rights, K0P/safety, required authority/consent, mandatory invariants and declared minimum evidence/assurance.
INFEASIBLE never enters compensatory ranking. UNKNOWN hard-feasibility remains UNKNOWN/ESCALATE/RESEARCH according to ComparePolicy, never FEASIBLE.

2. TYPED COMPARE RESULT
CompareResult contains separate fields:
  epistemic_result
  design_quality_result
  constraint_status
  adoption_recommendation
  per_dimension_results
  unresolved_questions
  policy_status
  provenance/trace.
These are distinct types and cannot substitute for one another.

constraint_status = FEASIBLE | INFEASIBLE | UNKNOWN | BLOCKED.
design_quality_result = DOMINATES | DOMINATED | EQUIVALENT | INCOMPARABLE | INCONCLUSIVE.
Per-dimension ordering = BETTER | WORSE | EQUIVALENT_WITHIN_POLICY | INCOMPARABLE | UNKNOWN.
Dominance means at least one BETTER and no WORSE among comparable soft dimensions after hard-feasibility; UNKNOWN on a decisive dimension yields INCONCLUSIVE rather than dominance.

Epistemic result independently records support appropriate to claim type; it never authorizes adoption.
Adoption recommendation is a proposal such as RETAIN | ADOPT_CANDIDATE_IF_AUTHORIZED | MERGE | RESEARCH | ESCALATE | REJECT. It is not an execution/admission token. Actual adoption remains governed by authority/AAP/DesignEpoch.
INFEASIBLE cannot recommend adoption.

3. NO DEFAULT SCALARIZATION
Default comparison is a partial order. Cross-dimension scalarization is forbidden unless a versioned ComparePolicy explicitly supplies a legitimate aggregation function, value/authority owner, units/normalization and hard-noncompensation constraints. Cost/speed/simplicity never compensate hard-gate failure.

4. COMPAREPOLICY FRESHNESS
ComparePolicy binds version, DesignEpoch/scope, validity, dimension definitions, units/frames, materiality/equivalence regions, uncertainty/dependence treatment, optional aggregation and escalation path.
Expired/mismatched/unresolved policy -> policy_status non-current and comparison becomes UNKNOWN/INCONCLUSIVE/ESCALATE as applicable; no silent default.
A proposal to change ComparePolicy or Engine.Compare protected evaluation semantics is evaluated using a frozen predecessor comparator/policy plus independent review/authority required by AAP. The new comparator cannot be sole judge of its own admission.

5. MANDATORY CLOSURE RECEIPT
Stage 1 emits RetrievalClosureReceipt identifying exact symbolic, mandatory dependency, applicable policy and invariant sets plus completeness/staleness status. Semantic TopK ranking is optional prioritization only. Incomplete mandatory closure -> UNKNOWN; absence in a partial retrieval is not absence-of-obligation.

6. COUNTERFACTUAL REPLAY
Replay binds:
  frozen operation/test set selected independently of proposal effects where feasible;
  information horizon / event time;
  source provenance;
  model/assumption/transportability refs;
  leakage controls;
  identification/materiality limits.
Information unavailable at historical time cannot leak into World A/B unless the counterfactual explicitly models that information intervention. Non-identifiable differences yield INCONCLUSIVE/NON_IDENTIFIABLE rather than forced ranking. Replay is model-derived evidence, not fresh empirical observation.

Candidate invariants:
- hard infeasibility is never compensated;
- four CompareResult channels remain separately typed;
- UNKNOWN decisive dimension cannot become dominance;
- stale ComparePolicy cannot silently execute;
- self-modified comparator cannot self-admit;
- partial mandatory retrieval cannot be treated as complete;
- replay future leakage is rejected.
