P012 SYNTHESIZED v15.9 CANDIDATE — ALGERBAS
Proposal-only; challenge this candidate against P012 source.

1. SIMPLIFY DECISION ALGEBRA
Decision Algebra is removed as an independent admitted universal Algebra because v15.8 supplies no independent operand/operator/law closure beyond typed decision/result semantics. Preserve its useful semantics as owner-qualified DecisionResult/DecisionEffect types consumed by Policy, Process and other engines. REG-ALGEBRA-001 therefore retains Process, Policy, Conformance, Evidence and Bridge/Representation algebras unless another packet independently justifies a sixth algebra. Migration aliases preserve historic Decision Algebra references; no semantic result type is lost.

2. POLICY ALGEBRA
Authority precedence is applied BEFORE any local ResolveStrategy. A lower-authority PERMIT_OVERRIDES can never override a higher-authority DENY/hard constraint.

NOT_D is not a general negation over operational PolicyDecision. Negation applies to typed policy predicates/conditions before decision lifting. Attempting NOT_D(PERMIT/DENY/REQUIRE_APPROVAL/DEFER/INDETERMINATE/CONFLICT/SAFE_FALLBACK) as an operational decision is MALFORMED unless an explicit registered decision-dual exists. This prevents DENY -> PERMIT manufacture.

AND_D / OR_D operate only over applicable decisions at the same resolved authority stratum after precedence/exception processing. NOT_APPLICABLE is contextual absence, not PERMIT. INDETERMINATE remains non-PASS. CONFLICT remains conflict until registered RESOLVE_D or escalation. REQUIRE_APPROVAL values compose as approval-requirement set union; no approval can be silently dropped.

If no valid ResolveStrategy applies, result = CONFLICT/ESCALATE, never PERMIT.

SAFE_FALLBACK is typed:
  SAFE_FALLBACK(trigger: FailureOrUncertaintyCondition, action_ref, authority_ref, scope, expiry)
It activates only when trigger occurs, must satisfy ordinary authority/rights/K0P/effect gates, and cannot widen triggering scope.

3. CONFORMANCE ALGEBRA
PASS requires complete applicable RequirementSet coverage, current required Evidence/Proof bindings and no unresolved mandatory GAP/UNKNOWN/STALE/BLOCKED. NOT_APPLICABLE is valid only after applicability resolution.
Waiver/exception is a typed authoritative input with scope/validity/provenance and becomes part of the requirement closure; it does not erase the original gap from history.
STALE evidence/proof contaminates only dependent obligations, not unrelated conformance dimensions.

4. EVIDENCE COMPOSITION ALGEBRA
Evidence composition separates:
  support/contradiction relation
  dependence structure
  uncertainty
  evidence credibility/applicability.
Shared-root/circular evidence cannot multiply support. Cyclic dependence is evaluated only under a declared convergent/fixed-point or otherwise terminating model; if no valid semantics exists -> BLOCKED/INCONCLUSIVE.
Detected dependence-model misspecification weakens to INCONCLUSIVE/CONDITIONAL and triggers re-estimation/research; it never increases confidence.
Conflicting evidence remains explicit rather than averaged away.
Probability/confidence output requires calibrated probabilistic model.

5. CROSS-ALGEBRA TYPING
PolicyDecision, ConformanceResult and EvidenceCompositionResult are distinct owner-qualified types. Similar labels (PASS/UNKNOWN/etc.) do not permit substitution. Cross-algebra conversion requires explicit adapter/bridge and cannot create truth or authority.

Candidate invariants:
- lower authority resolution never overrides higher hard constraint;
- operational decision negation cannot manufacture PERMIT;
- incomplete mandatory conformance cannot PASS;
- shared/cyclic evidence cannot inflate independent support;
- cross-algebra status labels are not implicit casts.
