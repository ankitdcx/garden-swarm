P014 SYNTHESIZED v15.9 CANDIDATE — ENGINE.REASON
Proposal-only; challenge this candidate against P014 source.

1. CLOSED REASON RESULT
ReasonResult contains:
  status = SOLVED | PARTIAL | INCONCLUSIVE | UNKNOWN | CONTRADICTION | RESOURCE_BOUND | MODEL_OUT_OF_SCOPE | VALIDATION_REQUIRED;
  claims/artifacts;
  problem_signature;
  strategy_trace;
  dependencies/provenance;
  tier;
  resource/termination receipt;
  ClaimValidationPlan where applicable.
All non-SOLVED states are non-authoritative. SOLVED means reasoning completed under its declared reasoning contract; it still does not mean proved, empirically true or authorized. Any required validation remains separately enforced.

2. R1–R4 ARE OBLIGATION CLASSES, NOT COMPUTE MULTIPLIERS
R1 — Local: bounded local reasoning, explicit assumptions/status, no claim of wider closure.
R2 — Contextual: resolve declared relevant context/dependencies/constraints and compare material alternatives.
R3 — Deep: broader strategy composition/simulation/formal/adversarial analysis as selected by AAP; explicit assumptions, cross-checks and validation plan.
R4 — Critical: maximum bounded reasoning assurance required by the operation, including independent challenge/diverse reasoning where policy requires and explicit escalation/governance/human route where applicable.
Domain name alone does not force R4; AAP derives tier from actual consequence, authority, uncertainty and applicable policy. More tokens alone never satisfy a higher tier.

3. PROBLEM SIGNATURE / PORTFOLIO SELECTION
ProblemSignature is produced independently of candidate strategy self-scores and binds problem class, semantic types, effect/authority sensitivity, causal/formal/empirical character, constraints, novelty/uncertainty, resource context and required validation classes.
Strategy selection uses Engine.Compare over admissible strategies and context-matched ReasoningStrategyPerformanceRecords.
A strategy/owner cannot be sole evaluator of the performance evidence that grants it higher assurance eligibility. Records label evaluator/source, held-out/leakage status, context similarity, DesignEpoch and uncertainty.
Self-graded evidence is retained but cannot independently satisfy protected selection/assurance floors.
Untried/new strategies may be explored only under an AAP-approved exploration policy; there is no mandatory novelty probe for every critical operation.

4. MIXED-STRATEGY COMPOSITION
Reason composition is a bounded typed DAG/process:
  strategy nodes have typed I/O;
  provenance/state handoffs are explicit;
  one top-level resource/termination budget applies;
  each node emits status/uncertainty;
  conflicts remain explicit.
A formal proof result or hard constraint is not averaged with an LLM/planner opinion. Contradictory outputs yield CONTRADICTION/INCONCLUSIVE until resolved by the applicable owner/validation path.
Composition trace records which strategy produced which claim.

5. CLAIMVALIDATIONPLAN
Consequential/promotable classification is derived from policy/AAP and claim/effect semantics outside the reasoner's self-confidence. Reason cannot mark a claim 'non-consequential' solely to bypass validation.
ClaimValidationPlan names required formal, empirical/test, legal/authority and human/governance routes with owners and status.
Reason-generated hypotheses/defaults remain claims/hypotheses, not Evidence.

6. ABSTRACTION / BACKWARD / CAUSAL REASONING
Every abstraction/preimage/backward result carries:
  model/context;
  soundness/approximation direction (over/under/exact/unknown as applicable);
  assumptions;
  dropped dimensions/invariants;
  validity scope;
  validation status.
Backward/preimage results are NECESSARY_CONDITION/WHY_NOT/HYPOTHESIS unless causal C0..C4 identification requirements are independently met. 'Root cause' wording is forbidden where only reachability/necessary-condition analysis was established.

7. PORTFOLIO EVOLUTION
Changes to protected evaluator metrics, admissible-context rules or assurance floors bind DesignEpoch/dependency invalidation and require independent evaluation/authority. Engine.Reason cannot self-certify a change to its own protected evaluation boundary.

Candidate invariants:
- Reason status never substitutes for Proof/Evidence/Authorization;
- R tier is demonstrated by obligations, not compute volume;
- self-grading cannot independently raise assurance eligibility;
- mixed-strategy conflicts are not silently averaged;
- Reason cannot self-classify consequential claim out of validation;
- preimage/abstraction cannot overclaim causality.
