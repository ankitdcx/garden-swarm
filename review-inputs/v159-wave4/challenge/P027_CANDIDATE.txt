GARDEN v15.9 — P027 SCT STRUCTURAL COHERENCE SYNTHESIZED CANDIDATE
Status: DESIGN CANDIDATE / NOT CANONICAL / NOT EXECUTABLY CERTIFIED

1. SCT SCORE PROFILE CONTRACT
Every SCT scoring profile SHALL declare:
- input discrepancy type/domain and reference scale;
- effective evidence-root/dependence handling;
- whether cardinality is semantically relevant;
- exact aggregation/normalization semantics;
- kappa domain/sign convention;
- variance definition;
- numerical-stability method;
- threshold calibration scope;
- INVALID / INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE / UNKNOWN behavior.

No profile may rely on incidental parser/item count. If cardinality is intended to affect the score, that effect and threshold calibration must be explicit. If cardinality is intended to be neutral, the profile must use a genuinely cardinality-canceling normalized aggregation, for example weighted log-mean-exp log(Σ w_i exp(kappa*d_i)/Σ w_i)/kappa, or another qualified equivalent. Merely normalizing weights without canceling the aggregation's cardinality term is insufficient. This candidate does not mandate one universal formula.

Empty input, all-zero effective evidence weight, nonfinite values, or parameters outside the profile's declared domain SHALL return a typed non-PASS result rather than a numeric score. Stable numerical evaluation is required; a nonfinite intermediate/output is INVALID, not evidence.

2. DEPENDENCE / DUPLICATE ROOTS
Before SCT evidence aggregation, materially shared provenance/common-root items SHALL be clustered or otherwise dependency-adjusted under the existing Evidence/CLIC dependence owner. Copying, summarizing, splitting or repeating a source SHALL NOT increase independent epistemic support merely by item count. UNKNOWN dependence remains UNKNOWN rather than independent-by-default.

3. COFAILURE != CORRELATION
Joint/co-error frequency and correlation are distinct typed statistics. A CofailureFrequency SHALL NOT be labelled or silently converted to ErrorCorrelation. If correlation is used, the profile SHALL bind estimator, marginals, sample window, uncertainty and zero-variance behavior. Heterogeneous statistics may not be max/averaged as though they shared a common scale unless a separately qualified mapping proves that interpretation.

4. HUMAN INPUT
Deprecate the SCT notation:
  HumanInput = EpistemicContribution × AuthorityContribution.
SCT SHALL alias the P018 HumanInputBundle semantics:
  epistemic contribution and authority/consent/rights contribution are disjoint typed channels.
Epistemic reliability may affect evidentiary weight. It SHALL NOT scale, dilute or manufacture rights, valid consent or legitimate authority. Authority/consent does not upgrade weak evidence to truth.

5. HARD ADMISSIBILITY BEFORE SOFT OPTIMIZATION
SCT optimization operates only on candidates that have already passed every applicable hard rights, safety, law, authority, consent and noncompensable bound.
A numerical/estimation/solver tolerance such as epsilon_band may apply only where the owning profile explicitly classifies the relevant bound as soft/compensable. It SHALL NOT widen a protected hard ceiling.
If uncertainty does not establish compliance below a protected hard/noncompensable ceiling, the result is the owner-qualified non-PASS state. Optimization cannot trade through the gate. Conservative resolution is mandatory at such hard gates rather than optional profile behavior.

6. THEORY-EQ BOUNDARY
All SCT equations remain conditional TheoryEquationContract instances. A reference equation does not prove empirical calibration, safety, correctness or universal applicability. Missing units, assumptions, validity domain, dependence semantics or falsifiers block qualified use.

PROVISIONAL INVARIANTS

SCT-159-002 Common-root copies SHALL NOT multiply independent evidence weight.
SCT-159-003 CofailureFrequency SHALL NOT masquerade as ErrorCorrelation.
SCT-159-004 Undefined/invalid arithmetic SHALL return typed non-PASS, never fabricated numeric PASS.
SCT-159-005 Epistemic reliability SHALL NOT scale rights/consent/authority.
SCT-159-006 Soft numerical tolerance SHALL NOT widen a protected hard gate.

PROVISIONAL TESTS
TEST-SCT-159-001 under a cardinality-neutral profile, the same declared discrepancy/weight distribution presented at n=1,3,10 after the profile's declared normalization SHALL produce the same score and threshold disposition within its declared numerical tolerance; any residual difference must come only from an explicitly declared cardinality term.
TEST-SCT-159-002 duplicate one evidence root ten times -> independent support does not increase.
TEST-SCT-159-003 identical rare failures vs independent common failures -> cofailure and correlation remain distinct.
TEST-SCT-159-004 empty/all-zero weights or invalid kappa/nonfinite input -> typed non-PASS.
TEST-SCT-159-005 low-reliability testimony + valid consent -> consent remains valid; high reliability + no authority -> no authority.
TEST-SCT-159-006 uncertainty interval crosses protected hard ceiling even if epsilon_band would admit it -> rejected/non-PASS before optimization.
