3. RELIABILITY RETRY BINDING
The Reliability OS generic retry budget is not an independent retry authority.

Retries are permitted only for operation classes whose current Execution/Process contract permits replay, including:
- read-only transient operations under the declared retry profile;
- verified same-effect idempotent operations under the current effect identity/fence contract, when the original effect is still required under current state/objective.

For a non-idempotent external effect with OUTCOME_UNKNOWN:
  blind replay is prohibited;
  reconcile or establish non-application before retry.

A retry-count threshold such as 'three retries' cannot override effect semantics.

4. FALLBACK DOES NOT LOWER HARD REQUIREMENTS
Provider/model/cache/fallback selection SHALL NOT weaken or bypass mandatory:
- authority;
- consent;
- rights;
- safety/K0P;
- privacy;
- law;
- required proof/verification;
- current effect/admission constraints.

This prohibition applies whether the weakening is silent or explicitly logged.
If no fallback satisfies the mandatory floor, the result is the owner-qualified non-PASS / degraded / minimum-risk route, not a lower-assurance PASS.

5. CONSEQUENTIAL CACHE / FALLBACK REVALIDATION
Before consequential use of cached/reused policy, evidence, answer, model output or fallback result, the consumer SHALL revalidate the current bindings required by its contract, including where applicable:
- authority;
- consent;
- privacy/purpose;
- evidence/knowledge currentness;
- DesignEpoch/configuration;
- invalidators;
- effect/admission state.

A current versioned contract may explicitly certify a reuse class whose qualifying bindings remain valid without a fresh remote lookup; absence of an observed invalidator is not itself authorization to skip the contract's required currentness check.

Failed revalidation -> STALE / UNKNOWN / REVALIDATION_REQUIRED / other owner-qualified non-PASS.

6. RELIABILITY OBJECTIVE DOMAIN
Reliability/churn/availability optimization operates only over the already-admissible option set:
  options satisfying all applicable current hard authority/consent/rights/safety/privacy/law/evidence/effect requirements before reliability optimization.

Reliability benefit cannot compensate for a hard-gate failure.

7. GESTALT / APAO
GESTALT coordination and APAO assurance outputs are typed coordination/assurance evidence. They do not mint authority, permission or proof by consensus. Any consequential use still binds separately valid authority and applicable assurance/proof owners.
