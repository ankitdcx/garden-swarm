1. DEFERRED / UNRESOLVED PROFILE RULE
Any profile/name explicitly marked DEFERRED or UNRESOLVED has no operational, authority, certification or requirement-satisfaction semantics merely from historical mention, neighboring similarity or acronym reuse.

This applies to the currently unresolved/deferred set including:
  UCAS
  Kindergarten
  CASI adapter activation
  GAC
  FDE
  CFBR
  GTAH
  unresolved manufacturing-qualification-transfer profiles
and any later equivalent status.

Historical references are archival/alias evidence only. Activation requires ordinary current:
  definition
  owner
  scope
  authority/admission
  proof/evidence
  DesignEpoch/migration
qualification as applicable.

A DEFERRED/UNRESOLVED name SHALL NOT silently fill a required capability/profile slot or satisfy an invariant by analogy.

2. CASI ACTIVATION
Historical CASI adapter mentions SHALL resolve to DEFERRED for current execution unless a later admitted source explicitly activates an exact adapter/profile version. No legacy reference, registry alias or previous candidate status may reactivate it implicitly.

3. QUALIFICATION / CERTIFICATION TRANSFER
Assurance, certification or qualification evidence is scoped to the exact source system/configuration/environment/profile for which it was established.

Transfer to manufacturing target, derivative, new environment or other context requires an explicit owner-qualified TransferAssessment / evidence-reuse decision that binds:
- source and target roots;
- claimed transferable properties;
- transfer assumptions;
- invalidators;
- changed dimensions;
- current evidence;
- result and unresolved obligations.

Missing transfer assumptions/invalidators or UNKNOWN applicability -> NOT_TRANSFERABLE / UNKNOWN / REQUALIFICATION_REQUIRED as owned by the active profile, never permission-by-default.

4. COMPARATIVE COGNITIVE COVERAGE
The Candidate-B/comparator programme remains:
  NOT_EXECUTED / UNRESOLVED
unless a later evidence package proves otherwise.

Any comparative conclusion requires at minimum:
- pinned artifact/version/configuration for every compared system;
- a predeclared common comparison protocol and dimensions;
- matched task/evidence conditions;
- applicability/uncertainty and missing-data rules;
- independence/dependence information for evaluators/evidence;
- retained raw/result provenance;
- explicit limitation that comparison does not create authority or universal superiority.

Missing any required comparison basis -> UNRESOLVED, not an inferred ranking.

The retained cross-tenant mutable-service covert-channel experiment and opaque-ID equivariance experiment remain bounded experiments/claims. This document does not promote them to external empirical results or theorems; promotion requires the ordinary evidence/review path.

5. TH15.8 SUMMARY ALIASES
TH15.8-1..14 are explanatory summary aliases to their detailed current owners (including the P022–P026 dependency families), not separate competing normative systems.

Each current-view summary SHALL identify its owning semantic anchor. On conflict, the admitted owner/version governs and the summary becomes STALE/INVALID until repaired. A summary SHALL NOT create an independent authority, proof or duplicate invariant merely by restating the concept.

6. CMUR INDEPENDENCE
CMUR retains: model count != independence.
Use the existing reviewer-independence/provenance owner rather than inventing one scalar independence metric. Material shared lineage/data/tooling/prompt/evaluator dependencies SHALL be disclosed and treated as correlated evidence where applicable. Correlated reviewers may still provide useful evidence but do not count as independent merely because they are separate calls/models.

7. REJECTION MEMORY
A rejection-memory entry retains its negative/rejected disposition, scope, reason, provenance and invalidators. Retrieval, migration, repetition or later reuse SHALL NOT silently convert it into positive evidence for promotion. Reconsideration requires a new version/evidence path; the historical rejection remains inspectable subject to privacy/lawful erasure.

8. REPRODUCTION / PROMOTION WORDING
Statements prohibiting autonomous reproduction do not themselves grant or define any reproduction, deployment, activation or promotion authority. Any consequential reproduction/deployment/promotion requires the ordinary applicable Research / Evolve / Proof / admission / legitimate-authority path and all other hard gates.

9. HISTORICAL OPERATIONAL FACTS
Historic budgets, prices, paused schedules, repository settings and operational observations remain dated evidence. They do not become current policy/theory axioms without a current verified source.
