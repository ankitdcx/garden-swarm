GARDEN v15.9 — P028 TRAIN / GAIA / DOMAIN PROFILE SYNTHESIZED CANDIDATE
Status: DESIGN CANDIDATE / NOT CANONICAL / NOT EXECUTABLY CERTIFIED

1. MINIMUM-RISK TRANSITION BINDING
Every TRAIN/Titanium/domain reference phrase such as:
  halt
  safe-halt
  disconnect
  watchdog fallback
  below-floor => halt
SHALL resolve to the active domain-qualified minimum-risk transition under the governing GSL-PHYSICS / Safety / Continuity / FET profile.

'Halt' does not universally mean zero output. Zero output is selected only where the qualified domain safety case establishes it as the appropriate minimum-risk action.

If the preferred nominal safe state is physically unreachable, the system SHALL:
- record assurance loss/degraded-emergency state;
- select the prequalified least-risk continuity/handoff/controlled-safe-state action available under current authority;
- preserve unresolved physical/effect state;
- not issue a 'guaranteed safe' claim merely because a fallback path executed.

2. ROBOTICS RECURSIVE FEASIBILITY
Present-step strict feasibility / Slater-style constraint qualification SHALL NOT by itself be described as guaranteeing recursive feasibility.

Recursive feasibility is a separate proof/qualification obligation under the selected:
- dynamics;
- disturbance set;
- constraint evolution;
- relative degree where relevant;
- actuator limits/slew;
- discretization;
- observation/calibration error;
- timing/WCET;
- composition assumptions;
- viability/reachable-set or qualified equivalent semantics.

A Slater-style condition may support the instantaneous optimization problem where mathematically appropriate, but does not discharge forward physical safety.

3. ROBOTICS FALLBACK SCOPE
A closed-form/simplified barrier fallback SHALL NOT be treated as qualified for a larger coupled physical problem unless the fallback evidence covers the actual active constraint set.

Qualification SHALL bind all materially active:
- barriers/constraints;
- actuator bounds;
- shared-resource/inter-robot effects;
- observation/timing assumptions;
- composition conditions.

If that coverage is absent or the intended safe state is unreachable, return the active owner-qualified assurance-loss/minimum-risk outcome rather than manufacturing a safety guarantee.

4. TIMING / PERFORMANCE VALUES
Reference values such as microsecond deadlines, fixed percentages or example thresholds remain illustrative/non-normative unless bound to exact:
- hardware/runtime/solver/model version;
- workload/constraint scale;
- measurement method;
- uncertainty/coverage;
- qualified domain profile.

The global illustrative-label rule applies locally as well; a copied profile excerpt must not turn a benchmark into a universal guarantee.

5. MEDIA v1.11
Latent curvature/smoothness regularization is a candidate optimization/training objective. It MAY improve temporal smoothness under declared decoder/metric/data assumptions.

It SHALL NOT by itself be claimed to ensure:
- identity persistence;
- no flicker;
- semantic consistency;
- rights/content-policy compliance.

Those are separately evaluated output-level properties under the appropriate identity, perception, policy, rights and safety owners.

6. DOMAIN PROFILE STATUS
MEDICAL, AUTOMOTIVE, POWER/GRID, CLOUD/DC, FINANCIAL, INDUSTRIAL, MEDIA and ROBOTICS remain profile/specification families. Local equations, constants, N-1 assumptions and claimed performance benefits are conditional on their declared model/evidence and do not become certification by inclusion in Garden.
