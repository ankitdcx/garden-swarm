GARDEN v15.9 — P029 VSM/RPM/PPM/TFT/FET/COMPOSITION SYNTHESIZED CANDIDATE
Status: KEEP-HEAVY DESIGN CANDIDATE / NOT CANONICAL / NOT EXECUTABLY CERTIFIED

DISPOSITION
The reviewed VSM, RPM, PPM, TFT, FET and theory-to-reality families retain their existing architecture. No new theory engine, evidence algebra, emergency authority source, identity rule or universal physical model is added.

1. VSM MERGE / CONTINUITY PROJECTION
VSM fork/merge SHALL preserve the source lineage of every materially contributing branch and SHALL NOT silently discard material uncertainty, invalidators, unresolved obligations or conflicting context merely to produce a single continuity representation.

The active owner-qualified merge policy determines how compatible uncertainty representations are combined. Where no valid conservative/compatible merge is defined, the result is typed UNKNOWN / CONFLICT / INCOMPATIBLE rather than a fabricated unified state.

VSM continuity/context/lineage scores are operational/model state. They SHALL NOT by themselves establish personhood, consciousness, ownership, rights or authority. Any such inference is a separately typed external claim under the applicable evidence/rights/authority process.

2. RPM / PPM COMPOSITION
Reactive and proactive regulation remain separate qualified components. When they jointly affect the same bounded actuator/resource, their composition SHALL be governed by an explicit owner-qualified joint envelope/arbitration or composition contract covering material saturation, delay, priority and conflict behavior.

If the applicable coupling is materially noncommutative, execution order or conflict-resolution semantics SHALL be declared. Absent a required composition rule, the combined result is non-PASS/UNKNOWN; commutativity or independence SHALL NOT be assumed merely because each component is individually qualified.

This does not impose one universal ordering across all RPM/PPM deployments.

3. TFT PROFILE BOUNDARY
TFT conservation/constitutive/transport statements remain profile- and domain-science-bound. Conservation SHALL NOT be inferred for a profile that does not declare and justify the relevant conserved quantity under its domain assumptions.

Information/graph influence, probability flow and physical material/energy transport remain semantically distinct; finite-speed or conservation conclusions do not silently transfer across these types.

4. FET AUTHORITY / UNKNOWN / RECOVERY
FET creates no emergency authority. Every emergency action is selected only from the intersection of:
- current legitimate authority / pre-authorized emergency grant;
- active safety/rights/law constraints;
- current emergency profile/action envelope.

UNKNOWN hazard/admission state remains typed non-PASS for any action requiring resolved admission. It SHALL NOT be consumed downstream as weak PASS.

FET recovery/handoff SHALL preserve applicable controller state/invalidators and shall not re-enable stale primary commands or accumulated controller state unless the active recovery profile explicitly requalifies them. Existing critical-continuity/safe-handoff owners remain authoritative; no duplicate recovery engine is created.

5. THEORY COUPLING DEFAULT
A coupling profile that materially depends on order, common cause, shared state or noncommutation must declare those semantics. If required dependence/order semantics are absent, the composition is unresolved/non-PASS rather than assumed independent/commutative.

6. THEORY-TO-REALITY / UPS
Theory/model/simulation output remains model-qualified until an observation/provenance path establishes a real-world evidence claim. UPS and DimensionSelectionProfile remain research hypotheses/profiles. Their outputs SHALL retain research/model provenance and cannot be promoted to discovered physical law by downstream repetition, retrieval or use.

7. UNDEFINED RESEARCH LABELS
A named research target lacking an admitted profile (for example a retained Gaia2026 label) remains a research label with no operational or certification semantics until its exact profile is admitted.
