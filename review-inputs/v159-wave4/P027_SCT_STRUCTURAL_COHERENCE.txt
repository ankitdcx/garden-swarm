KNOWN LOCAL WITNESSES TO CHALLENGE, NOT ASSUME CORRECT
1. If every discrepancy is zero and kappa=1, the unnormalized log-sum term gives 0 for n=1, about 1.0986 for n=3 and about 2.3026 for n=10. A threshold can therefore change solely with input count unless cardinality is intentionally part of the profile.
2. Joint failure frequency is not the same quantity as correlation: identical rare binary failures can have joint frequency 0.02 but correlation 1; independent 0.5 failure indicators have joint frequency 0.25 but correlation 0.
3. A positive epsilon band can numerically admit risk above an independently hard ceiling if the implementation applies the formula literally.
