P027 — SCT STRUCTURAL COHERENCE
Expected material questions:
- The retained coherence score needs explicit cardinality/normalization semantics; zero discrepancy should not accidentally worsen only because parser/source count grows unless the profile intentionally models cardinality.
- Joint co-error frequency and correlation are different statistics; dependence must retain typed meaning, marginals, estimator and uncertainty.
- Soft numerical/estimation bands must not widen a hard safety/rights/authority ceiling. Optimization domain should already satisfy mandatory hard gates.
- Human epistemic contribution and human authority/consent are disjoint channels; reliability cannot scale rights or authority. P018 already repairs this globally, so SCT should alias that semantics rather than create a second construct.
- Reference equations stay conditional under THEORY-EQ; underspecified formula -> non-PASS, not fake numeric certainty.

P028 — TRAIN / GAIA / DOMAIN PROFILES
Expected material questions:
- Every local 'halt', 'safe-halt', watchdog/disconnect shorthand must resolve to the domain-qualified minimum-risk transition. Blind zero-output is not universally safe.
- Robotics single-step Slater/strict feasibility does not prove recursive feasibility. Future feasibility requires dynamics/disturbance/constraint/timing viability evidence.
- Closed-form fallback must cover the actual coupled constraint set and verified worst-case timing; illustrative microsecond claims remain profile/evidence-bound.
- MEDIA curvature regularization can support smoothness but cannot by itself guarantee identity preservation or semantic/content-policy compliance.
- Reference constants/claims remain profile-specific and unvalidated unless receipts exist; hardware veto and physical hard floors are preserved.

- Preserve privacy, consent, rights, law, safety and human agency.
