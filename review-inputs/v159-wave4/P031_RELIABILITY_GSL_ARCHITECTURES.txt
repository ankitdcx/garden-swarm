RELIABILITY OBJECTIVE BOUNDARY
Low churn/high availability/reliability is an optimization objective only inside the already-admissible option set. It cannot compensate for a violated rights/safety/authority/privacy hard gate.

RETRY / FALLBACK BOUNDARY
- Read-only transient operations may use a bounded retry profile when their contract permits it.
- Unknown non-idempotent external effects require reconciliation/established non-application before replay.
- Same-effect idempotent operations may be safely replayed only under the current effect identity/fence contract.
- Provider/model fallback cannot silently weaken mandatory verification or expand authority.
- Cached answer/policy/evidence reuse must revalidate freshness, evidence, authority, privacy, DesignEpoch and invalidators when required.

MIGRATION / REPLACEMENT BOUNDARY
Moving from one GSL architecture/reference profile to another requires ordinary versioned DesignBinding/migration/compatibility checks. Shared names or claimed efficiency do not prove semantic equivalence.
