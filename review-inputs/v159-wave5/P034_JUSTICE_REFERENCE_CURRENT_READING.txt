SOURCE — ABBREVIATED JUSTICE FLOW TO REVIEW
T15.8-16 contains a compact sequence similar to:
RECEIVED -> PRESERVATION_HOLD -> CONFLICT_SCREENED -> ... -> PROSECUTION_OR_CLAIM | CLOSED_WITH_REASON -> ADJUDICATION -> APPEAL -> ENFORCEMENT -> ARCHIVED_WITH_REOPEN_CONDITIONS.
Read literally, this can make every report receive a hold before conflict screening, make CLOSED_WITH_REASON continue to adjudication/enforcement, make appeal mandatory rather than available, and omit the explicit defense/representation stage present in retained System justice workflow.

PROPOSED GUARDED GRAPH SEMANTICS TO CHALLENGE
- The flow is a proceeding-profile transition graph, not an unconditional chain.
- At intake, conflict screening and time-sensitive preservation may proceed in parallel when justified.
- A preservation hold requires applicable purpose, lawful authority, scope, minimum-necessary data, independence/review where required, expiry/review and release/deletion conditions. Allegation alone grants none.
- A preauthorized urgent preservation path may precede complete nonurgent screening only inside its bounded grant, with prompt independent review.
- Investigation, evidence request, obstruction review, claim/prosecution, defense/representation, adjudication, remedy, appeal, enforcement and archive transitions are enabled only by the applicable proceeding profile.
- CLOSED_WITH_REASON routes to review/archive/reopening as authorized, not automatically to adjudication/enforcement.
- Appeal availability does not mean every case must enter appeal.
- Enforcement requires a valid decision and applicable finality/stay status.
- Incomplete/missing evidence never becomes guilt merely because the process advanced.

JUSTICE CANDIDATE ARTIFACTS
EvidenceControlRisk:
  should assess evidence/subject/controller refs, control powers, conflicts/dependencies, preservation risk, independent access/custody options, authority/privacy constraints, uncertainty and risk result. It creates no guilt inference.

ObstructionEvent:
  should bind a valid duty/hold and its service/scope, observed conduct, preservation chain, explanation/uncertainty and independently adjudicated outcome. Missing/destroyed evidence alone does not prove knowing obstruction or the underlying offence.

JusticeEvidenceContinuityReceipt:
  should bind case/evidence scope, custody transfers, holds, lawful disclosure/retention permissions, missing/contested evidence, state reason, next accountable owner/deadline and reopen conditions. It must not claim continuity if material evidence is missing.
