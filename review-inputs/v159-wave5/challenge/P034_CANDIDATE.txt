GARDEN v15.9 — P034 JUSTICE / CURRENT READING / REFERENCE CLOSURE SYNTHESIZED CANDIDATE
Status: DESIGN CANDIDATE / NOT JURISDICTION-SPECIFIC LEGAL CERTIFICATION / NOT CANONICAL

1. JUSTICE FLOW IS A GUARDED TRANSITION GRAPH
The compact retained justice sequence is a non-exhaustive projection, not an unconditional chain.
`JusticeProceedingProfile` here is an annotation/reference pattern over the existing applicable law/governance/proceeding owner or qualified equivalent; it is not a new top-level justice schema and does not replace an existing proceeding owner.
The referenced proceeding profile binds at minimum:
- proceeding type and jurisdiction/applicability;
- lawful authority anchor and competent owner;
- purpose/scope;
- allowed states/transitions;
- evidence/disclosure/retention rules;
- conflict/recusal rules;
- defense/representation requirements where applicable;
- standards/finality/stay/appeal/remedy/enforcement rules;
- deadlines/review intervals where law/profile requires them;
- invalidators/version/currentness.

A transition is enabled only when its profile applicability/authority is resolved and current. UNKNOWN potentially mandatory authority/applicability -> non-PASS.

2. INTAKE / CONFLICT / PRESERVATION
At intake, conflict screening and time-sensitive preservation MAY proceed in parallel where the applicable profile permits or where a documented, reviewable justification exists under the profile's current authority. Any such justification is recorded on the hold and does not create new authority.
A preservation hold requires:
- lawful purpose/authority;
- defined evidence/data scope;
- minimum-necessary retention/access;
- custody/provenance;
- independent/conflict-free review where required;
- explicit expiry/review condition;
- release/deletion/renewal rule;
- privacy/lawful-erasure conflict disposition.

An allegation alone grants no hold authority.

3. URGENT PRESERVATION
A pre-authorized urgent preservation path may precede complete nonurgent screening only inside its existing bounded grant.
The urgent hold SHALL carry owner/profile-defined:
- exigency trigger/evidence;
- maximum duration/expiry;
- independent review deadline where required;
- scope/access limitations;
- release/deletion/further-processing disposition if review/renewal does not occur.

No universal 24/48/72-hour constant is introduced; applicable law/proceeding profile owns deadlines. Expiry does not self-renew. Renewal requires current competent authority, a fresh re-evaluation of the exigency conditions, and the profile's current evidence/review conditions; mere procedural continuation is insufficient.

4. TERMINAL / OPTIONAL / COERCIVE STATES
CLOSED_WITH_REASON is terminal by default for the active proceeding instance.
A stored reopen condition is only a trigger for review; actual reopening requires a new current authorized decision under the applicable profile.

APPEAL is an optional/profile-enabled transition, not an automatic mandatory state. It exists only when the applicable profile provides an appeal/review path and the entitled party/trigger invokes it as required.

ENFORCEMENT requires:
- a valid enforceable decision/remedy/order;
- current authority;
- applicable finality/stay status;
- any required notice/representation/appeal conditions.
No linear fall-through from CLOSED_WITH_REASON, mere allegation or incomplete evidence to enforcement.

5. DEFENSE / REPRESENTATION
Defense/representation is a guarded due-process transition/opportunity when required by the applicable proceeding/law/rights profile. Where the profile requires that opportunity before a consequential stage, the transition to that consequential stage is non-PASS until the required opportunity and its profile-defined access/disclosure conditions are satisfied or a profile-authorized exception is validly established.
This candidate does not invent one universal timing or disclosure rule across jurisdictions. The profile must explicitly encode the required opportunity, access/disclosure constraints, conflict/privilege/privacy rules and consequences of non-satisfaction.

6. EVIDENCE CONTROL RISK
EvidenceControlRisk is procedural/evidentiary-risk assessment, not offence evidence and not guilt probability.
It binds the evidence/subject/controller scope, relevant control powers/dependencies/conflicts, preservation/access risk, independent custody/access alternatives, authority/privacy constraints, uncertainty and disposition.
Any interface displaying, logging, or exporting EvidenceControlRisk SHALL carry a persistent non-removable semantic label equivalent to `PROCEDURAL RISK ONLY — NOT GUILT EVIDENCE`, and SHALL NOT use the score/result as direct actor-specific wrongdoing evidence.

7. OBSTRUCTION EVENT
ObstructionEvent is not established by missing/destroyed evidence alone.
An adjudicable obstruction claim requires the applicable law/profile's evidence elements, which may include as relevant:
- existence and service/knowledge of a valid duty/hold;
- actor-specific conduct/omission;
- temporal/causal relation to the duty;
- required intent/knowledge state under applicable law;
- alternative explanation/uncertainty;
- competent independent adjudication.
No universal criminal elements are invented; the owner profile supplies them. Pending allegation and adjudicated outcome remain distinct.

8. JUSTICE EVIDENCE CONTINUITY
JusticeEvidenceContinuityReceipt is a custody/lineage/case-state refinement or extension of existing Evidence/Incident/Custody owners unless P032 host comparison proves a distinct persistent schema is necessary.
It binds case/evidence scope, custody transfers, active holds/authority, lawful disclosure/retention, missing/contested evidence, continuity state/reason, next accountable owner/deadline and reopen/review conditions.
If material evidence is missing/unknown, it SHALL NOT claim complete continuity.

EvidenceControlRisk and ObstructionEvent likewise default to refinements/annotations over existing EvidenceThreatProfile / IncidentRecord / ObservationRecord owners rather than new top-level justice entities until schema closure.

9. CURRENT READING MAP
v15.9 SHALL provide a derived CurrentReadingMap generated from the final source/overlay set.
It is a navigation/interpretation artifact, not a new authority registry.
Required fields per entry:
- concept/qualified name;
- current effective owner/name;
- current anchor(s) and source hash/version;
- current status;
- historical aliases/owners retained for citation;
- retained-history snapshot/hash or equivalent immutable lineage anchor where applicable;
- supersession/navigation relation where applicable;
- EXTERNAL/UNRESOLVED dependency refs;
- resolution owner/review condition for unresolved refs.

Every active primary current link must resolve to an exact source/overlay anchor. If the anchor cannot be proved, mark EXTERNAL/UNRESOLVED; do not emit an invented active link.
The existing overlay/release owner owns deterministic map regeneration/review as part of release/current-reading closure; no new registry owner is created. The release binds the generated map hash so consumers can detect stale maps.

On conflict between a derived map and the underlying current overlay/source, the admitted source/overlay owner governs and the map defect is recorded/rebuilt.

10. HISTORICAL OWNER DIFFERENCES
When historical and current owner/name differ:
- current effective owner governs current semantics only when a source-anchored supersession exists;
- historical owner/name remains citation/lineage information;
- no authority transfers merely because an alias changed.

Engine.Orchestrate historical references are preserved for lineage. The final CurrentReadingMap may resolve them to the reviewed Engine.Reason/orchestration ownership only when the exact reviewed source/overlay anchor is included in the release; otherwise mark UNRESOLVED rather than inventing a new top-level engine.
Any future new orchestration owner requires an explicit reviewed supersession, not accidental alias drift.

11. TRUNCATED / DANGLING HISTORICAL REFERENCES
Historical retained dangling tokens such as truncated `A-` may remain inside immutable predecessor bytes.
The current-reading layer SHALL NOT use them as active anchors. It resolves to the complete current invariant/test family where source-proven, or marks the active reference UNRESOLVED.

PROVISIONAL INVARIANTS
P34-001 Justice proceeding transitions SHALL be profile/authority gated, not unconditional linear fall-through.
P34-002 Allegation alone SHALL NOT create preservation or coercive authority.
P34-003 Urgent preservation SHALL be bounded by explicit profile-owned expiry/review/release rules, cannot self-renew, and renewal SHALL require fresh exigency evaluation.
P34-004 CLOSED_WITH_REASON SHALL not automatically route to adjudication/enforcement.
P34-005 APPEAL SHALL be optional/profile-enabled and ENFORCEMENT SHALL require finality/stay/authority checks.
P34-006 Missing evidence or evidentiary-control risk SHALL NOT become guilt evidence by itself.
P34-007 Obstruction allegation and adjudicated obstruction outcome SHALL remain distinct.
P34-008 Justice candidate artifacts SHALL prefer existing Evidence/Incident/Custody hosts until P032 proves need for new schemas.
P34-009 CurrentReadingMap SHALL be derived/source-anchored, hash-bound to the release, and SHALL NOT manufacture current owners.
P34-010 Historical alias/supersession SHALL NOT transfer authority by naming alone.
P34-011 A transition to a consequential stage for which representation is a required due-process condition SHALL be non-PASS while the profile-required representation opportunity remains unsatisfied.
