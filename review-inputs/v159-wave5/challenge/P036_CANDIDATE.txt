4. STATUS MODEL
Preserve source `existing_status` domain:

Add a separate closure/knowledge field when status itself cannot yet be established:
  closure_status = RESOLVED | UNKNOWN | STALE | CONFLICT | INCOMPLETE | NEEDS_SCHEMA_ADMISSION | BLOCKED.
Do not overload existing_status with UNKNOWN/STALE/CONFLICT if the source enum does not contain them.

  delta_status = ENFORCED | CHECKED | SPECIFIED | NOT_PRESENT | UNKNOWN | STALE | CONFLICT | INCOMPLETE | NEEDS_SCHEMA_ADMISSION | BLOCKED.
UNKNOWN, STALE, CONFLICT, INCOMPLETE, NEEDS_SCHEMA_ADMISSION and BLOCKED are non-PASS wherever hard resolution/admission is required. Only exact evidence can justify ENFORCED/CHECKED.
