# Canonical LICENSE change policy

## Purpose

Keep the repository's current `LICENSE` stable and authoritative while allowing future improvements to be considered without silently changing the terms.

## Rules

1. **Canonical baseline.** The LICENSE on the default branch is the repository's canonical use-terms text. Existing wording remains in force within the repository until a change is expressly approved and merged.
2. **Proposals are separate.** New wording, alternatives, and policy experiments belong in a proposal document or a pull request that clearly identifies itself as proposed. They must not be presented as current terms.
3. **Express owner approval.** No LICENSE change is adopted without the repository owner's express approval of the specific text being merged.
4. **Review for merit.** Evaluate each proposal for coherence, fairness, practical feasibility, clarity, unintended consequences, consistency with existing terms, and whether it is a meaningful improvement. A proposal may be revised, deferred, or rejected.
5. **Traceable history.** Record the proposal, its rationale, review comments, decision, accepted wording, and rollback history in version control.
6. **No automatic upgrades.** Newer dates, version numbers, model agreement, or the existence of a proposal do not make it canonical.
7. **README consistency.** The README must point to the LICENSE as the current text and identify proposal documents as non-binding proposals.
8. **Scope.** This is a repository governance policy. Calling a text canonical describes its status in the project; it does not by itself establish legal enforceability in every jurisdiction.

## Current decision
