## Secondary channels

After the core technical surface is stronger, consider targeted posts or submissions to:

- AI safety newsletters and research digests;
- agent-security and formal-verification communities;
- r/MachineLearning, r/LocalLLaMA, r/artificial, r/AIAlignment and relevant open-source communities, subject to each community's rules;
- X/Threads and LinkedIn through project-branded accounts;
- podcasts/newsletters focused on agent systems, AI control, governance or formal assurance.

## Prioritization

### Now

1. Curated governance/agent lists that accept source-design projects.
2. Independent evaluators who can produce public technical findings.
3. Framework examples that can operate read-only against the public source.

### After live A2A/MCP exists

1. A2A registries.
2. MCP Registry.
3. Agent interoperability demos.
4. Paid directory placement only if measurable discovery/conversion exists.

### After executable GSL / evaluation harness exists

1. Developer-framework integrations.
2. Research benchmarks.
3. Larger launch channels and newsletters.
4. Conference/workshop submissions where technically appropriate.

## Outreach metric

Record for each target:

- submission date;
- exact artifact/link;
- response/listing status;
- visits or inbound requests where measurable;
- external finding/PR/integration generated;
- follow-up date;
- whether continued outreach is worth the cost.

A listing that generates no serious engagement is less valuable than one external reproducible evaluation.
