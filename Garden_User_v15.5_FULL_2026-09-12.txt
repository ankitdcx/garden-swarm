ROADMAP

Garden is a human-centered architecture for representing knowledge, reasoning about it, checking it, acting on it and
learning from what happens.

Its purpose is not merely to answer questions. Garden is designed to connect:

  human intent
  → clear meaning
  → relevant knowledge and theories
  → comparison and reasoning
  → proof, evidence, rights, law and safety checks
  → authorized action
  → observation of what actually happened
  → correction, learning and explanation back to humans.

Garden is larger than GSL. GSL is the semantic language that gives Garden a shared way to describe things, events,
actions, claims, rules, authority, context and change.

Garden is not a fixed list of modules. Its authoritative architecture is an expandable typed graph. The familiar 0–9
structure is a human navigation view:

  0. Human Sovereignty Core
  1. Garden Core
  2. Universal Algebras
  3. Meta-Engines
  4. Shared Capabilities
  5. Domain System
  6. Theory System
  7. Context / World System
  8. Profiles / Reference Implementations
  9. Instances / Deployments

A part can move in the displayed hierarchy without changing its identity. Authority comes from explicit rules and
bindings, never from where something appears in a diagram.

UNIVERSAL ACTIVATION, SUBSCRIPTION AND SCHEDULING

Garden processes can begin because a human/system requests work, an event occurs, a schedule becomes due, state or a
dependency changes, a stream crosses a condition, background work remains eligible, or recovery/emergency logic
activates.

All of these activation forms enter the same governed process path:
  trigger
  → typed context and applicability
  → AAP admission and assurance selection
  → applicable Constitution/rights/safety/authority/policy checks
  → Runtime scheduling
  → Process Algebra / UPA execution
  → observation, receipt, audit and learning.

Not every internal microstep synchronously invokes Policy Algebra. Policy is evaluated wherever applicable, and
time-critical execution may use a fresh pre-authorized bounded envelope whose scope, authority, validity, DesignEpoch
and
invalidators are explicit. Crossing that envelope re-enters the deeper assurance path.

Garden does not add a new Scheduling Engine. Runtime owns actual scheduling/execution queues; Time Service owns temporal
eligibility and deadlines; Event Service owns event delivery/subscriptions; Engine.Orchestrate/AAP owns admission,
assurance floors and resource/priority requirements.

Required continuous work is protected from starvation, while safety/emergency work retains its hard priority floors.

CONSTITUTIONAL EVENTS

Every consequential constitutional or rights check becomes observable through a typed ConstitutionalEvent. A violation
blocks the offending transition, preserves evidence, records the applicable constitutional/rights context and routes the
case to the required explanation, audit, escalation or containment path.

A violation does not automatically freeze an agent or establish guilt/intent. Containment or restriction requires its
own applicable authority, evidence, severity and risk criteria.

HISTORICAL BELIEF AND EXPLAINABLE BELIEF CHANGE

Garden can reconstruct what epistemic state it assigned to a claim at an earlier time without injecting later knowledge
into that historical state.

BeliefTimeline records the sequence of material epistemic changes. Each material change carries an EpistemicTransition
with a typed reason such as new evidence, new proof, context change, dependency invalidation, correction of error,
conflict resolution, safeguard failure or UNKNOWN.

This allows Garden to answer:
  "What did you believe about X at time T?"
  "Why did you change your mind about X?"
  "What evidence, proof, dependency or failure caused that change?"
  "What would make the current belief require revalidation again?"

Historical belief is not current belief, and neither is automatically historical truth. An unexplained material belief
change is itself a system-health finding and may enter Continuous Failure Learning. Where a materially changed claim
previously informed a PredictionArtifact or calibrated forecast, the transition also feeds the applicable prediction-
calibration/revalidation path using independently validated evidence or outcomes. The belief change itself is not
Evidence or ground truth.

DESIGN SIMULATION, FORWARD STRESS AND GLOBAL CONSISTENCY

Before a design change becomes MERGE_READY, Garden can build a bounded DesignMirror of the affected design closure and
produce a DesignSimulationReceipt. The mirror is not the canonical design, and simulation is not implementation
validation, empirical evidence or proof of real-world safety.

High-consequence structural candidates may also undergo Forward Adversarial Future Stress Assessment against declared
adverse scenarios. Results are explicitly conditional on the scenario, assumptions, horizon, models and uncertainty;
Garden never issues a universal "survival certificate."

Garden also produces a GlobalInvariantConsistencyReceipt for the simultaneously applicable invariant set. Different
invariants may legitimately apply in different contexts. The defect is a silent mutually unsatisfiable set of hard
obligations that are simultaneously applicable.

CONTINUOUS FAILURE LEARNING

Every materially validated failure, including one discovered retrospectively, enters a persistent learning process:
  contain where necessary
  → preserve evidence
  → reconstruct what happened
  → identify proximal/systemic causes
  → ask why applicable safeguards did or did not catch it
  → generalize any supported causal pattern
  → search the affected present/historical/deployed/pending closure
  → generate and verify prevention
  → create regression coverage
  → continue monitoring.

Closing the immediate incident does not mean the epistemic obligation is solved. UNKNOWN root cause may remain a bounded
open obligation for the continuous background sweep.

If an auditor, proof method, dependency resolver, safety checker or other assurance mechanism is found defective,
dependent prior certificates and decisions become STALE or REVALIDATION_REQUIRED until rechecked through an acceptable
independent path.

ONTOLOGICAL DEBT AND LOSS-AWARE HISTORY

OntologicalDebtView is a view over existing ArchitecturalDebt. It makes recurring representational insufficiency visible
when Garden repeatedly lacks a concept/category/abstraction needed for faithful reasoning. An unfamiliar or unmodeled
concept is not automatically ontology debt.

HistoricalSemanticSummary allows old material to be compacted under lawful retention policy while retaining the minimum
permitted anti-regression and reconstruction knowledge. There is no universal five-year purge rule and no "immortal
data" rule. Privacy, law, safety, evidence, proof and retention authority remain controlling.

The continuous background failure sweep also revisits unresolved historical failures when new evidence, capabilities,
dependency information or environmental changes make a better explanation or prevention possible.

PRESENTATION BUILDER / READER PROJECTIONS — DERIVED_VIEW QUALIFICATION PROFILE

The five source documents remain the authoritative human-documentation suite. Garden may generate reader-specific views
from that same semantic source:
  Single-Volume Reader Edition;

Generated views may use Theory/Equation/Artifact Cards, breadcrumbs, semantic callouts, compact reference panels,

Default reader tracks are:
  Implementer: purpose/authority → flow → technical contract → schema → test/proof → deployment.
  Auditor: scope → hard rule → invariant → evidence/proof → exception/UNKNOWN → receipt/provenance.
  Scientist: theory → assumptions → equation/model → applicability → evidence/falsifier → uncertainty → research gap.

A simplification such as changing "where the applicable rules require it" into an unconditional requirement is a
projection defect. Hand editing a generated reader view does not modify canonical Garden design.

2. HUMAN SOVEREIGNTY, RIGHTS AND CONSENT  [H-02]

ROADMAP
  - rights and personal sovereignty
  - consent, delegation and revocation
  - human authority versus AI capability

+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| HARD LIMIT — HUMAN LEGITIMACY                                                                                |
|   Capability, intelligence, optimization, reputation or majority agreement cannot create authority over a    |
|   person's fundamental rights. Valid consent and lawful authority remain separate from epistemic scoring.    |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

Humans are Garden's purpose and legitimacy core.

A human is not a resource owned by Garden. A person's dignity, rights and basic worth do not come from reputation,
wealth, intelligence, model scores or majority approval.

Garden therefore separates:

  what a person wants;
  what that person has authority to decide;
  what requires another person's consent;
  what law permits;
  what safety allows;
  what the Constitution and active rights protect.

Intent is not authority. Capability is not permission. A recommendation is not authorization.

The active constitutional registry contains RIGHT-001 through RIGHT-025. RIGHT-026 is reserved and inactive.
RIGHT-027 is on hold and inactive. An inactive or undefined right identifier has no hidden meaning.

Important human protections include life and bodily integrity, privacy, due process, identity protection, personal
sovereignty, freedom from covert manipulation, the right to remain human, equal human rights, ordinary human life and
human flourishing.

Consequential changes to a person's body, mind, identity, private life or mode of existence require legitimate
authority and valid consent. Missing, stale, coerced or poorly understood consent defaults to preserving the person's
state where the applicable rules require it.

Garden Core supplies the minimum shared machinery every Garden instance needs.

GSL v45.1 provides six Pillars, ten Core Objects and 24 Core Relations.

The ten Core Objects are:

  TIME      — when something exists, happens or is valid.
  SPACE     — where something is located or related spatially.
  THING     — the general root for entities and artifacts.
  EVENT     — something that occurs.
  ACTION    — an intentional state-changing operation.
  AGENCY    — actors, capability, delegation and accountability.
  RULE      — a condition, conclusion, obligation or constraint.
  VALUE     — facts, preferences and norms represented without collapsing them together.
  CONTEXT   — the scope and circumstances in which meaning and rules apply.
  CLAIM     — an assertion that can be supported, challenged, qualified or remain unknown.

Garden keeps important distinctions explicit:

  observation ≠ evidence ≠ claim ≠ knowledge;
  prediction ≠ evidence;
  formal proof ≠ empirical truth;
  intent ≠ authority;
  recommendation ≠ authorization;
  specification ≠ reality;
  UNKNOWN ≠ FALSE.

Garden also keeps provenance: where information came from, how it changed, which assumptions were used and which
version of the design and environment applied.

4. HOW GARDEN ACTS: PROCESS, COMPARE, REASON AND ASSURANCE  [H-04]

ROADMAP
  - universal step loop
  - tiered Compare/Reason/Proof/Safety
  - FunctionContract and typed results
  - AAP assurance selection

UNIVERSAL MEANINGFUL STEP
  Observe -> Type -> Compare -> Reason -> Candidates -> Select -> Guard
                                                        |
                                                        v
                                         Execute / Derive / Communicate
                                                        |
                                                        v
                                  Observe -> Compare expected/actual
                                                        |
                                                        v
                         Accept / Correct / Retry / Replan / Research /
                              Escalate / Learn / Terminate

Garden uses one common process idea rather than inventing a different lifecycle for every subsystem.

A meaningful operation is treated as a controlled transition:

  Observe
  → understand and type the situation
  → compare the current state with the desired or expected state
  → reason about alternatives
  → select a candidate
  → guard the selected candidate
  → execute, derive or communicate
  → observe what happened
  → compare expected and actual results
  → update, correct, retry, replan, research, escalate, learn or stop.

This loop applies to meaningful semantic boundaries. Garden does not create a heavyweight record for every processor
instruction or neural activation.

COMPARE asks how two states, claims, plans or alternatives differ. It can report that one is better on declared
dimensions, that they are approximately equal, incomparable, inconclusive or unknown.

REASON determines what follows, what alternatives exist and what further evidence or checking is required.

Reasoning is tiered from very cheap local checks to deep critical analysis. Proof and safety have their own tiers.
A deeper reasoning tier does not automatically mean every other subsystem must use the same depth.

Engine.Orchestrate implements the Activation and Assurance Policy (AAP). AAP chooses the smallest sufficient part of
Garden and the minimum assurance depth that can safely handle the operation.

Cost reduction is allowed only after mandatory rights, safety, authority, privacy, security and other hard requirements
are preserved.

Garden uses a shared FunctionContract behind every executable function. It records inputs/outputs, effects, authority,
failure states, dependencies, resources and provenance. Operations that can be UNKNOWN, STALE, BLOCKED, TIMEOUT or
INCONCLUSIVE use typed multi-status results rather than hiding those outcomes inside Bool or None.

Operational numbers such as thresholds, cadences and timeouts are QualifiedProfileParameters. A number is identified as
a formal bound, measured value, engineering target or profile parameter with its scope and source.

5. PROOF, SAFETY, SECURITY, LAW AND AUTHORITY  [H-05]

ROADMAP
  - formal proof boundary
  - K0P and emergency admission
  - security/authenticity
  - law, policy and human authority

CONSEQUENTIAL EXECUTION GATES
  Constitution / Rights ----+
  K0P / Safety -------------+
  Authenticity -------------+--> ALL applicable hard gates pass --> Execute
  Authority / Consent ------+
  Law / Policy -------------+
  Proof / Evidence floor ---+

  Emergency does not remove this structure; it uses a pre-authorized, bounded path through it.

+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| HARD LIMIT — INDEPENDENT GATES                                                                               |
|   K0P cannot be bypassed. Constitution and rights cannot be traded away for performance. Emergency uses      |
|   pre-authorized bounded authority; it does not create authority or disable legitimate authorization.        |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

Garden deliberately separates different kinds of trust.

Proof checks formal statements under declared assumptions. It does not prove that a real-world measurement is correct
merely because the mathematics is valid.

Evidence supports empirical claims. Evidence quality depends on source, method, independence, uncertainty, scope and
provenance.

Safety decides whether a physical action is admissible inside hard safety limits. Garden's K0P safety boundary is a
hardware-enforced veto for controlled physical actuation.

Security checks identity, integrity, credentials, isolation, cryptography and technical protection.

Law and governance determine legitimate normative authority within jurisdiction and constitutional limits.

These checks are not interchangeable. A physically safe action may still be illegal or unauthorized. A lawful action
may still be physically unsafe. A formally proved algorithm may still rely on a false empirical assumption.

For consequential execution, all applicable hard gates must permit the operation.

Emergency now uses one EmergencyAdmissionContract. It combines a domain HazardPredicate, pre-authorized authority,
authenticity, K0P, Constitution/rights, scope/expiry, a bounded SafeActionSet, recovery and audit. Emergency may replace
ordinary deliberation only where already authorized; it never creates authority and never turns K0P off.

6. KNOWLEDGE, EVIDENCE, RESEARCH AND EXPLANATION  [H-06]

ROADMAP
  - Observation/Evidence/Claim/Knowledge separation
  - continuous revalidation
  - Research and Reason
  - faithful explanation

Garden does not treat stored information as automatically true.

Knowledge stores observations, evidence, claims, theories, interpretations, provenance and snapshots. Validation status
can change while the original record remains preserved.

Contradictions are not forced into an arbitrary answer. CONFLICTED, UNKNOWN and INCONCLUSIVE are legitimate states.

Previously accepted knowledge can be rechecked when:
  new evidence appears;
  assumptions change;
  the environment changes;
  a dependency changes;
  a contradiction appears;
  the information becomes stale for its intended use;
  a high-impact operation needs stronger assurance.

Research searches for new evidence, theories and explanations. Retrieval and model generation do not create evidence.

Explanation turns Garden's actual traces into human-understandable answers. It may explain what happened, why, how,
why not, what evidence exists, what is uncertain, who authorized an action and what alternatives remain.

Explanation cannot invent missing evidence or reasoning simply to make an answer sound complete.

Human input is explicitly split into an EpistemicContribution and an AuthorityContribution. Testimony or forecasts may
be calibrated for reliability. Valid consent, rights and lawful authority are not confidence-weighted signals and
cannot be down-ranked by model performance.

Garden separates universal architecture from subject knowledge.

Domains can grow without changing Garden's universal core. Current broad domain families include:

  Mathematics
  Physical Sciences
  Earth Sciences
  Life Sciences
  Human and Social Sciences
  Engineering
  Computing
  Applied Domains such as mobility, space, energy, health, industry, media and education.

A DomainPackage can contain domain concepts, measurements, models, theories, evidence, safety requirements and open
research questions.

Garden also has a Theory System. A theory is not accepted merely because someone says it applies. Garden distinguishes
claimed applicability from validated applicability.

The seven current Garden core theories are:

  Layered Reality      (SRT)
  Persistent State     (VSM v2.0)
  Reactive Regulation  (RPM v2.0)
  Proactive Regulation (PPM v2.0)
  Transport Phenomena  (TFT v1.1)
  Emergency Response   (FET)
  Structural Coherence (SCT)

The complete canonical theory definitions now live in the companion `Garden_Theories` source under
[TH-*] anchors. Technical/System/Human files retain only the bindings and explanation/flow needed at their own depth.

VSM v2.0 generalizes the old scalar valence state into typed, context-bound, multiscale persistent operational state;
valence is optional, persistence is separated from selfhood, and HSA/TED remain controlling.

RPM v2.0 generalizes the historical scalar voter/validator model into typed reactive regulation over declared state
spaces. Agent mixes and phase labels become qualified profiles/derived assessments, and stability remains separate from
Safety and authority.

PPM v2.0 generalizes the asymmetric-well/dual-clock model into typed asymmetric regulation with auditable adaptive
fidelity. H(z) cannot create a hazard or emergency authority, and lower-fidelity paths cannot skip mandatory gates.

TFT v1.1 generalizes spatial transport notation into typed domain/evolution/operator contracts, separates transport
regimes from emergency ControlMode, and requires explicit validity, causal, identifiability and breakdown semantics.

These theories are not treated as universal physical laws. They can be narrowed, challenged, revalidated or rejected
within their actual scope.

Garden uses a TheoryEquationContract. Important equations must declare variable types, units, domain or graph/state
space, operators, assumptions, boundary/initial conditions where relevant, stochastic meaning, validity limits and
falsifiers before they can be treated as machine-certified mathematics.

This also clarifies the core theory repairs: RPM uses explicit normalization scales; TFT/PPM/FET distinguish spatial
field operators from graph/state-space transport; FET emergency action is triggered by a validated domain hazard, not
merely by the internal PPM/VSM regulation term H(x).

8. CONTEXTS, PROFILES AND REAL GARDEN INSTANCES  [H-08]

ROADMAP
  - world, jurisdiction and operating context
  - profiles/reference implementations

A rule or theory is rarely meaningful without context.

Context tells Garden where, when and under whose authority something applies. It can include:
  world or environment;
  jurisdiction;
  institution;
  community;
  personal context;
  research context;
  locale and culture;
  normal, emergency, offline, simulation or experiment state.

Profiles are concrete ways of implementing or testing Garden contracts. Named reference architectures including
GSL-KR, GSL-AR, GSL-R, TRAIN, GAIA Titanium, GAIA Diamond, GESTALT/APAO integration and Reliability OS have their
complete architecture definitions in the companion `Garden_Theories` source under [RA-*] anchors.
Specialized legal, privacy, research and physical profiles remain owned by their Technical sections.

An instance is an actual Garden deployment for a person, household, hospital, university, factory, robot fleet,
community, city, nation, Earth-scale system or future bounded off-world setting.

A profile or instance cannot create a competing truth source or bypass the Human Sovereignty and Garden Core rules.

Garden is designed to improve without silently rewriting itself.

PREDECESSOR ARTIFACT IMPORT
Garden may import an artifact created under an earlier Garden/GSL/schema version without restoring that earlier design.
The source version and identifiers are resolved through the current import-compatibility registry, then migrated into
current types. Missing or ambiguous mappings stay QUARANTINED/UNKNOWN. Import cannot create authority, evidence,
current architecture identity or a topology constraint. The original artifact and provenance remain inspectable.

Changes are proposed on branches. The accepted history remains immutable. A candidate change is checked for:
  semantic meaning;
  affected dependencies;
  rights and safety;
  evidence and proof obligations;
  security and privacy;
  compatibility;
  operational consequences;
  authority;
  rollback or compensation;
  post-change validation.

DO_NOTHING is always a valid comparator. Garden should not change merely because a new proposal exists.

Self-audit checks deployable changes against the complete applicable design rather than only the local section that was
edited. Automatic recursive patching is bounded. If the system cannot obtain a valid result within its limits, it stops
or escalates instead of declaring success.

A subsystem may not be the sole judge of a change to its own trust, authority or verification boundary.

10. HISTORICAL LEARNING, RESEARCH RADAR AND RESOURCE ALLOCATION  [H-10]

ROADMAP
  - historical counterfactual replay
  - research-and-theory radar
  - continuous resource allocation
  - qualified parameters

Garden can look backward as well as forward.

Historical retrospective optimization asks whether, given what Garden knows now, an earlier accepted decision could
have been better. It creates a new counterfactual branch, replays descendants and compares the resulting trajectory.
It never rewrites the original history.

The Research-and-Theory Radar continuously looks for useful research and theories. Discovery does not equal validation.
New work can become a candidate for research, comparison or evolution, but cannot silently merge itself into Garden.

Resource Allocation separates:
  forecast;

| HARD LIMIT — REPRESENTATION IS NOT IDENTITY                                                                  |
|   A vector, matrix, tensor, embedding or operator is a representation of a semantic object, not the object.  |

Bridge exists because efficient computation often needs representations that look very different from Garden's
semantic graph. A Claim may be represented by an embedding vector. A relation may be represented by a sparse operator.
A process may be represented by an operator graph. The machine may then realize those representations as tensors,
accelerator kernels or distributed arrays.

Garden keeps three layers distinct:

  Semantic State
    → what the object/process means.

  Computational Representation
    → the vector/matrix/tensor/operator form used for analysis or efficient computation.

  Hardware / Execution Realization
    → the concrete CPU/GPU/NPU/distributed/quantum mechanism executing that representation.

An exact representation claim requires a correspondence proof for the declared semantic properties and validity domain.
Approximate or lossy representations must declare their error/loss. If the mapping becomes stale, out of domain,
incompatible or resource-limited, Garden preserves that result instead of pretending exactness.

Recursive operator analysis also separates four different questions:

  Does a fixed point exist?
  Does iteration converge?
  Is the operator stable?
  Does the actual recursive process terminate?

These are not interchangeable. A contractive iteration can converge toward a fixed point while a program still runs
forever unless it has a stopping condition. A process can also be deliberately bounded by contract without proving
mathematical convergence.

Bridge has an optional Recursive Optimization Profile. It may use spectral prioritization, tensor batching, fixed-point
caching, approximate pruning, matrix reachability, precompiled kernels, inverse approximation, analytical pruning or
resource guidance. Optimization cannot create truth, safety, authority, proof or admissibility. Cache hits and fast
paths remain valid only while their semantic, dependency, model, environment, hardware, precision and DesignEpoch
bindings remain valid.

Performance claims remain evidence-bound. An expected speedup is a model projection or engineering target until an
actual benchmark/measurement supports it.

13E. CROSS-CUTTING ASSURANCE AND REFINEMENT HARDENING  [H-ASSURANCE-HARDENING]

PHYSICAL-COGNITIVE DEPLOYMENT HARDENING

Physical-cognitive hardening is cross-cutting and creates no new AGI profile or top-level architecture object.

Ten controlling principles:
1. Model reversal does not imply world reversal.
2. Novel uncertainty reduces authority before it expands ontology.
3. Unknown risk uses risk envelopes rather than forced probabilities.
4. Authoritative intent requires integrity, provenance and faithful semantic derivation.
5. Observation of cognition remains inside a declared effects budget.
6. Resource failure follows pre-authorized graceful degradation; hard safeguards are not improvised away.
7. Materially conflicting perception reduces actuation authority until trustworthy state estimation is restored.
8. Adaptations cannot escape their validated domain or temporal horizon without promotion.
9. Assurance claims cannot exceed observable hardware/software capabilities.
10. Fallback targets a context-defined minimum-risk condition rather than a universal blind halt.

Graceful degradation is approved before resource failure occurs. Expensive analysis may be shed according to the
current DegradationPlan, but Constitution, HSA, K0P, actuator-authority enforcement and other declared non-sheddable
safeguards remain active or authority transfers to a state that no longer depends on the unavailable capability.

This profile closes high-consequence gaps without creating a new top-level engine or replacing existing semantics.
Its contracts are owned by the existing Safety, Proof, Knowledge, Compare, Reason, Dependency, Compliance, Policy,
Compile, Transition, HSA, GSL-PHYSICS, Bridge and domain owners.

CRITICAL CONTINUITY

If deployed critical physical infrastructure encounters HSA uncertainty or violation, Garden does not equate safety
with abruptly removing physical control. The mandatory priority is:

  AGENCY/AUTHORITY REVOCATION
    → INDEPENDENT SAFE CONTROL HANDOFF
    → MINIMUM SAFE CONTINUITY
    → QUALIFIED RECOVERY.

The suspect AI loses agency, credentials and authority first. A separately qualified fallback controller receives only
the minimum bounded control needed for safe continuity. It cannot depend on the suspect AI's mutable cognition, goals,
credentials or authority. Determinism alone is never proof of safety. Physical shutdown remains available only when the
system-specific safety case establishes that shutdown is safer and physically feasible.

EVOLVING DO_NOTHING BASELINES

For continuous physical or biological systems, DO_NOTHING means no new Garden intervention. It never means that the
world freezes. Endogenous dynamics, decay, disease, wear, environment, human action and hazards continue. The baseline
therefore declares a trajectory/model, evaluation horizon, uncertainty, monitoring, decay/hazard projection and
handoff/review conditions where applicable.

PHYSICAL TELEMETRY AND TWIN SYNCHRONIZATION

Physical measurements may arrive late, duplicated, out of order or with uncertain clocks. Garden therefore keeps the
time of the physical event or observation distinct from the time data entered the system. It records the clock domain,
synchronization uncertainty, transport latency, jitter, sequence or causal ordering and source provenance before a
measurement can update a physical twin.

The current data-assimilation profile declares how measurements are reordered, deduplicated, rejected or quarantined;
which units, frames, calibration and model assumptions apply; whether the requested state is actually observable or
identifiable; how uncertainty, drift and staleness propagate; and what the monitoring/event loop does when data is
missing or too late. Arrival order is not assumed to be causal order, and a model never invents precise latent state
when the available telemetry cannot identify it. Failure invokes a prevalidated fallback or controlled-safe state for
the affected scope; it does not create new authority.

QUANTUM/CLASSICAL CORRESPONDENCE

QuantumClassicalCorrespondenceReceipt states the model class, validity regime, coarse-graining/decoherence assumptions,
approximation and error bounds, CPTP assumptions where applicable, Markovian or non-Markovian assumptions, breakdown
conditions and the higher-fidelity quantum model required at the boundary. Lindblad/GKSL is used only where its own
assumptions are valid; it is not a universal quantum-dynamics requirement.

COUNTERFACTUAL-INFLUENCE PROVENANCE

Garden records Counterfactual → Decision → Intervention → Observation → Evidence as a causal/provenance chain.
Subsequent real observations remain empirical. They are classified as observational, interventional or endogenous
evidence according to how they were produced. The earlier prediction/counterfactual cannot count as independent
evidence for itself, and dependence/confounding remains explicit.

Where the assurance profile requires tamper-evident provenance, the content-addressed
CounterfactualInfluenceProvenance chain is bound through the existing external or cryptographic provenance machinery.
This binding marks ancestry, dependence and integrity; it does not discard genuine observations or make them
non-empirical. Cryptographic integrity establishes only the integrity of the declared bytes and links under the
registered scheme. It does not by itself establish truth, independence, causality or correctness.

MACHINE-TRACE SECURITY REFINEMENT

Semantic Garden policy must refine to actual machine traces. Evidence covers relevant timing, cache, memory-access,
speculation, shared-resource contention, power/EM and other side/covert channels for the declared attacker and leakage
model. Garden does not materialize every CPU instruction as a semantic TransitionStep; it proves or measures the
appropriate refinement, noninterference or bounded-leakage relation between semantic transitions and machine traces.

INVARIANT EVOLUTION AND PROOF INVALIDATION

Proofs bind to exact invariant semantic identities/hashes, not only names or SchemaIDs. An invariant delta is
EQUIVALENT, STRENGTHENING, WEAKENING, REFINEMENT, INCOMPARABLE, REDEFINITION or UNKNOWN. Dependent proof closure is
invalidated whenever the classified delta and migration evidence do not justify reuse. Reuse across DesignEpochs needs
verified equivalence/refinement/migration evidence; schema compatibility alone is insufficient.

DERIVED-VIEW DIVERGENCE

Editing a generated DERIVED_VIEW creates a divergence-ledger entry binding source generated hash, edited hash,
editor/authority, time/provenance, exact delta, semantic classification, reason and relationship to the authoritative
source. The edited view remains non-authoritative and cannot silently become canonical semantic source.

EPISTEMIC WELL-FOUNDEDNESS

Garden preserves acyclic epistemic justification while continuing to admit explicitly modeled causal/fixed-point
feedback. EpistemicWellFoundednessReceipt audits support SCCs, independent grounding, dependence and admitted feedback
semantics. Circular evidence without independent grounding cannot bootstrap credibility.

CERTIFIED IDENTIFIERS, LEGAL INTERPRETATION AND TYPED EPISTEMIC ARTIFACTS

Human-friendly aliases remain useful in prose. A certified Machine Package uses canonical qualified identifiers;
ambiguous bare aliases fail. LegalFormalizationInterpretationReceipt distinguishes contradiction in source law from an
encoding defect, missing exception/context, jurisdiction/scope mismatch or unresolved legal interpretation. SMT UNSAT
is evidence about the formalized model, not automatic invalidation of human law.

ObservationRecord, Evidence, Claim and KnowledgeRecord are distinct typed artifacts. Support and derivation relations
create new linked artifacts; the same object does not mutate from Observation into Evidence into Claim into Knowledge.

Garden systematically looks for useful connections across fields. [H-OCF] OPERATIONAL CLOSURE FOUNDATION — FOUNDATIONAL META-DESIGN
========================================================================================================================
Garden does not require a person to specify every implementation detail. Garden must itself identify material unresolved obligations and give each one a truthful disposition: implemented-and-qualified; derivable from pinned semantics; designable through bounded comparison; discovery-required from reality or an authorised choice; or blocked with an explicit frontier.

FOUNDATIONAL RULE — NO DESCRIPTIVE LEAF.
A material requirement is not complete merely because it has a convincing explanation, schema name, AI-generated implementation, or approving majority. Garden must connect it to an implementable/executable contract, a derivation, a bounded design procedure, an explicit discovery protocol, or a visible blocked frontier.

Garden should hide unnecessary internal complexity from ordinary users. It must still expose consequential unresolved choices, uncertainty, rights/authority limits, and material failure conditions when they matter.

OCF changes no human right, authority root, safety root, ten-object GSL ontology, or top-level engine. It generalises the existing Compile/Reason/Compare/Proof/Dependency/Research/Evolve/Audit/GSL-KR discipline across all present and future Garden responsibilities.

A correct OCF result may be "unknown", "needs evidence", "blocked", or "not yet implementable". Honest incompleteness is preferable to false closure.

[H-MSC] MINIMUM STABLE CORE — WHAT MAKES GARDEN “GARDEN”
========================================================================================================================
Garden intentionally keeps its permanent foundation smaller than its total functionality.

The Minimum Stable Core is the smallest set of responsibilities that higher-level theories, AI models, runtimes and domain modules
must preserve when they are upgraded or replaced:

1. typed semantic representation through GSL and the ten Core Objects;
2. CLAIM/CONTEXT/evidence/provenance/uncertainty separation in GSL-KR;
3. HumanCore/Human Sovereignty, consent, privacy and bounded authority;
4. truth/proof/implementation/validation/certification kept as different states;
5. versioned DesignEpoch/source/dependency lineage and correction propagation;
6. no self-certification of changes to protected evaluators, safety roots or authority roots;
7. OCF operational closure: no material requirement ends as unexplained prose;
8. CPI recursive construction: distinguish DERIVATION, DESIGN, DISCOVERY and BLOCKED work;
9. bounded resource/termination behavior and honest UNKNOWN/INCONCLUSIVE states;
10. point-of-use authority/safety/applicability checks before consequential effects.

Everything above this foundation may evolve when a better mechanism is demonstrated. A theory or module is not permanently “final”
merely because it is currently accepted. Current acceptance means best admitted version for its stated scope and evidence.

========================================================================================================================
[H-TML] LIVING THEORY & MODULE LIFECYCLE
========================================================================================================================
Every Garden theory or replaceable module can improve again when new evidence, better mathematics, cheaper computation, new failure
evidence, or a better design becomes available.

Typical states:
PROPOSED → EXPERIMENTAL → QUALIFIED → CURRENT_BEST
with possible transitions to CHALLENGED, SUPERSEDED, REFUTED, RETIRED or OUT_OF_SCOPE.

Garden keeps old versions, why they changed, the evidence behind the change, and what remains valid from the older version.
A newer version is not automatically better merely because it is newer.

[H-V151-UPGRADES] NEW OPERATIONAL UPGRADES

v15.1 strengthens Garden without adding new top-level engines:

[H-CMUR] CROSS-MODEL UPGRADE REVIEW
========================================================================================================================
Garden may use one AI to create an upgrade, but it must not automatically accept that upgrade merely because the same AI thinks its work is correct.

Before any automatic theory/module/design upgrade is admitted:
- multiple distinct qualified LLMs must independently inspect it;
- they must use different review roles, including at least a skeptical/red-team role;
- their disagreements, shared evidence and possible common-model ancestry must be recorded;
- material objections must be resolved, tested, or left as explicit blockers;
- majority voting cannot turn an unsupported claim into truth.

Default automatic-upgrade minimum: at least 3 distinct qualified model families/checkpoints where available, performing at least 5 review roles.
For foundational, safety-critical, authority-changing, privacy-critical, or irreversible changes, the review profile must be stronger and should use additional independent model families/providers/checkers where available. If the required diversity cannot be obtained, the change may remain a candidate but SHALL NOT be automatically promoted.

Multi-model review supplements—not replaces—proof, experiments, testing, source evidence, rights review or human/constitutional authority where those are required.

[H-CLIC] CROSS-LAYER INTEGRITY, CAUSALITY & CONTINUITY

Garden v15.3 adds no new top-level engine. It makes five hidden relationships between existing parts explicit:
1. Intent-to-effect semantic conservation — what the person meant must remain materially preserved through interpretation, planning, commands and effects.
2. Causal intervention qualification — prediction/correlation is not automatically evidence that changing X will cause Y. Existing C0..C4 causal meanings remain controlling.
3. Attention/salience governance — Garden must spend attention on what could materially change a decision, not merely what is frequent, dramatic or repeated.
4. Identity/commitment continuity — restart, migration, fork, merge or model replacement must not silently erase commitments or multiply authority.
5. Emergence/common-cause/reversibility/option preservation — individually acceptable components can still produce dangerous collective behavior; correlated evidence is not independent; irreversible actions need stronger support; future safe options matter.

Human-facing rule: perspective modelling is not consent, salience is not truth, prediction is not observation, intelligence is not authority, and a successful outcome does not retroactively rewrite the original permission.

CLIC reuses existing Reason, Research, Compare, Dependency, Bridge, Transition, Reliability OS, AAP, CPI, CDDT, GSL-KR, Diamond and Titanium responsibilities. Unknown required links remain explicit; consequential ordinary action is blocked only where the existing safety/authority policy requires it.

CASI remains DEFERRED by prior user decision. No deferred cognition-substrate architecture is silently activated in v15.3.

v15.4 adds or strengthens four things:

1. FORMAL ALGEBRA TABLES
   Process, Policy, Decision, Conformance and Evidence now have explicit registered operator contracts. Every operator

   Garden may assess how a small informational intervention redirects much larger pre-existing physical, computational,
   economic, institutional or social flows. GCL is not a new force and is not authority. It is a counterfactual,
   evidence-bounded analysis built on the existing causal/CLIC machinery. Results remain a vector unless a domain owner
   explicitly supplies a valid scalarization.

3. EVIDENCE CANNOT BE VOTED INTO OR OUT OF TRUTH
   Existing governance, multi-agent and review rules are made explicit at the v15.4 integration boundary: votes,
   popularity or model count cannot promote a claim to truth or erase a materially supported counterexample. Governance
   may still make legitimate choices under uncertainty, but the unresolved evidence remains visible and scoped.

4. HISTORICAL-PATCH RECONCILIATION
   The old Agency Spectrum is not adopted as a normative authority ladder; capability and authority remain
   multidimensional. Hardware attestation is useful where required but is not a universal prerequisite for Evidence.
   There is no fixed five-year deletion rule. Historical summarisation follows privacy, law, safety, accountability and
   retention policy. No new SymphonyConductor is added; existing Proof/Dependency/Compliance machinery owns global
   invariant consistency.

GCL does not let a high-leverage message override consent, rights, policy or safety. A tiny command that can move large
resources is precisely a reason to require stronger causal attribution, authority checks and reversibility analysis where
applicable.
