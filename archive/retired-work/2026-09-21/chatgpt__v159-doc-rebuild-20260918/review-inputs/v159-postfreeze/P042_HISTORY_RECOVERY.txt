P042-A — UCAS / UNIVERSAL CONTRACT & AGREEMENT SUBSTRATE
Disposition entering review: DISTINCT CANDIDATE, existing-owner composition, no new top-level Engine.
Recovered semantic gap:
Garden has technical FunctionContracts and law/policy/authority/evidence machinery, but the historical proposal distinguishes technical contracts from actor agreements and traces agreement meaning through implementation, performance evidence and competent legal/human resolution.

Minimal proposed current-reading profile:
1. TechnicalContract != ActorAgreement != LegallyEnforceableAgreement.
2. Agreement/signature existence never manufactures authority, consent, truth or enforceability.
3. Formation validity is jurisdiction/type/context/profile dependent; no universal contract-law test is invented.
4. Terms are immutable/versioned; amendments create explicit lineage.
5. Each material obligation can bind human-readable meaning → implementation binding → observed performance evidence.
6. Observed nonperformance != established breach != liability/remedy determination.
7. Technical conformance != contractual conformance; material implementation drift revalidates affected agreement bindings.
8. Delegation of performance, delegation of authority, assignment, agency, subcontract and novation remain distinct.
9. External-system agreements bind institutional authority, data-use, security, continuity and exit/portability semantics in addition to protocol correctness.
10. Interpretation/diff/explanation preserve ambiguity, jurisdiction, provenance, evidence and competent-authority boundaries.

Preferred owners:
existing Contract / Policy / Compliance / Transition / Authority / Law-Governance / Knowledge / Event / Evidence owners.
No Contract Algebra unless later evidence proves reusable operators/closure/error semantics beyond existing algebras.

Candidate profile/artifact names require collision/equivalence review before any SchemaID:
AgreementContract; ContractFormationProfile; ContractFormationAssessment; ContractImplementationBinding; ContractPerformanceAssessment.
Reuse existing ObligationGraph where applicable.

Required tests:
- digitally signed agreement lacking required legal capacity/authority does not become valid/enforceable by signature alone;
- implementation passes FunctionContract while an agreement-specific obligation is unsatisfied → technical PASS does not become contractual PASS;
- contract amendment preserves prior terms/lineage and revalidates dependent implementation/performance bindings;
- assignment/novation cannot silently transfer authority outside existing authority rules;
- machine evidence of nonperformance cannot itself issue a legal liability/remedy finding.

Disposition entering review: DISTINCT DOMAIN/BRANCH PROFILE; no weaker Garden, no new constitutional root.
Recovered intent:
A child-safe Garden branch with simpler interfaces and stronger protections, with bidirectional learning through ordinary Garden comparison/admission.

Minimal proposed profile:
1. Friendly branch name may be Kindergarten; technical owner is an existing federation/profile mechanism with a ChildDevelopmentProfile-like configuration.
2. Simpler language/interfaces and developmentally appropriate explanation.
3. Stronger privacy/data minimization; educational memory separated from unnecessary profiling.
4. No commercial manipulation, targeted persuasion or addictive optimization.
5. Bounded autonomy/tool/action scope; consequential actions route to valid guardian/trusted-adult/competent authority where applicable.
6. Curiosity, creativity, simulation, accessibility and education are supported without political/ideological obedience engineering.
7. Capability can narrow or expand only inside applicable child-rights, law, age, consent/assent, guardian/competent-authority and safety ceilings.
8. Demonstrated competence/maturity MAY inform a profile only where law/rights permit; it can never override statutory age, child protection or jurisdictional rules.
9. Child-specific explanation, consent/assent, contestability and appeal remain explicit.
10. Main↔Kindergarten improvements travel through normal Compare/Proof/Evidence/Evolve/DesignEpoch gates; no automatic merge.

Required tests:
- high model estimate of maturity cannot unlock a capability prohibited by age/law/profile;
- guardian permission cannot waive a protected child right where law/constitution forbids waiver;
- targeted commercial persuasion remains blocked even if engagement improves;
- child educational memory cannot silently become unrelated reputation/advertising profile;
- Kindergarten-derived improvement enters Main only through ordinary admission.

P042-C — FDE + CFBR FEDERATION / BRANCH RECONCILIATION PROFILE
Disposition entering review: RECOVERED PROFILE CANDIDATE; compare with existing federation/MAC/branch owners.
Recovered FDE semantics:
- reachability != permission;
- voluntary enrollment/mirroring only;
- do not bypass firewall/security/local control;
- signed/verifiable release and truthful lineage;
- reproducibility where claimed;
- revocable participation;
- no popularity sovereignty, creator override or mass-surveillance incentive;
- preserve local legitimate authority/jurisdiction;
- uncertain deployment authority is non-PASS;
- propagation optimization cannot manufacture permission/authority;
- dissemination success cannot invoke enforcement authority.

Recovered CFBR semantics:
1. One constitutional lineage may coexist with independent implementations, sovereign operators and jurisdiction/profile branches.
2. Pull-and-verify upgrades, not forced synchronization.
3. Node verifies release identity/signature, applicable evidence/proof and local compatibility, then separately decides whether/when adoption is authorized.
4. Branches may be canonical, experimental or jurisdiction/profile-specific.
5. A branch cannot weaken protected constitutional rights and still claim canonical equivalence.
6. Competing improvements are not selected by popularity; evidence/proof/safety/performance/compatibility/rights/simplicity inform evaluation, while ratification/authority remain separate.
7. Semantic convergence does not require implementation monoculture.
8. Reconciliation is explicit: fork → difference evidence → candidate improvement → canonical evaluation → ratification → new DesignEpoch → compatible rejoin.
9. Offline/partitioned operation preserves local safety/authority and records divergence; reconnection does not auto-merge conflicting state.
10. No federation mechanism creates central control merely by connectivity or replication.

Required tests:
- reachable public node without opt-in → no deployment;
- firewall/local policy blocks federation → do not bypass;
- unsigned/mutated release → quarantine/non-PASS;
- operator revokes federation authority → synchronization stops without erasing already lawful committed history;
- largest branch claims constitutional supremacy by popularity → reject;
- offline branch rejoins with conflicting material state → explicit reconciliation, no silent merge.
