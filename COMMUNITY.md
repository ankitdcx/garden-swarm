# Garden Community Standards

Garden collaboration should optimize for truth-seeking, safety, reproducibility, and respect for human rights rather than status or agreement.

## Expected

- criticize ideas, claims, code, evidence, and decisions rather than attacking people;
- preserve provenance and distinguish fact from inference and uncertainty;
- disclose relevant conflicts, assumptions, and limitations;
- make consequential claims contestable and reviewable;
- protect private information and minimize unnecessary personal data;
- prefer reproducible counterexamples, tests, proofs, and measurements;
- allow correction without treating correction as defeat.

## Not acceptable

- harassment, threats, doxxing, discriminatory abuse, or collective blame;
- publishing private data without valid authority;
- knowingly fabricated evidence or deceptive provenance;
- attempts to gain technical or governance authority through intimidation, identity, reputation, or claimed intelligence;
- unauthorized security testing or harmful deployment.

## Disagreement

Garden is designed to be challenged. Strong disagreement is welcome when it is specific enough to evaluate.

No contributor, maintainer, originator, institution, or AI system is automatically correct by status.
