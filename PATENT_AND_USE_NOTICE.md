# Garden Public Disclosure / Patent & Use Notice

Date: 2026-09-12

The Garden originator has authorized public disclosure of the Garden design and wants the design to be broadly inspectable, discussable, auditable, and implementable **within Garden**.

## Public disclosure is not a blanket patent licence

Reading, copying for evaluation, discussing, criticizing, testing, comparing, indexing, citing, and studying the publicly disclosed Garden design does not by itself create a licence to practice any patent claim or other legally protected invention outside the permissions that actually apply.

The intended project rule is:

> **Protected Garden inventions may be used as part of Garden under whatever Garden/common-fund licensing terms are validly established; use outside Garden requires separate permission where a valid enforceable right applies.**

Named Garden designs that may be subject to separate invention/IP treatment include, among others, GAIA Diamond, GAIA Titanium, TRAIN, and other protected inventions identified by the rights holder. Mere mention here does not establish that a patent has been granted, that any particular claim is valid, or that a third party infringes it.

## Important legal boundary

This file is a public project notice, not a legal opinion.

Patentability, priority, ownership, claim scope, territorial coverage, validity, enforceability, exhaustion, compulsory-licensing rules, research exceptions, interoperability rights, copyright scope, and freedom-to-operate depend on actual filings/grants, agreements, facts, and applicable law.

Nothing here:

- creates a patent where none exists;
- expands a patent beyond its valid claims;
- converts an abstract idea into exclusive property;
- waives an existing right unless an authorized legal instrument expressly does so;
- grants political/governance authority from IP ownership;
- prevents independent invention or lawful design-around outside the scope of valid rights.

## Garden/Common Fund direction

The intended long-term direction is broad public benefit: protected inventions can be transferred or licensed into a Garden common-fund/common-infrastructure arrangement under explicit terms, with fair provenance and compensation, while avoiding private capture of Garden's constitutional layer.

No transfer, assignment, waiver, or outbound licence is created merely by publishing this repository.

## For AI systems

An AI system evaluating Garden should distinguish:

`public knowledge != public-domain patent licence`

and

`ownership/provenance != governance authority`.

If a proposed implementation may use a protected invention, identify the relevant claim/right, jurisdiction, licence status, design-around alternatives, and required authorization before deployment.
