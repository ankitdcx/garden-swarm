# GAIA terminology

Status: **Garden v15.6 candidate terminology — not part of published Garden v15.5**.

For the Garden v15.6 candidate line, **GAIA** expands to:

> **Garden Artificial Intelligence Architecture**

This expansion applies to the existing reference-architecture names **GAIA Titanium** and **GAIA Diamond**.

The word **Artificial** is intentional. GAIA covers the broader Garden AI architecture, including safety, assurance and other components that are not themselves autonomous agents. Expanding the `A` as `Autonomous` would narrow the meaning incorrectly.

This is a terminology clarification only. It does **not** change GAIA Titanium or GAIA Diamond behavior, contracts, safety/assurance semantics, authority, HSA, topology, or DesignEpoch rules.

Published Garden v15.5 remains immutable. If this candidate passes the normal successor-review gates, the complete Garden v15.6 five-file candidate should introduce the expansion at the first normative GAIA definition and keep subsequent `GAIA Titanium` / `GAIA Diamond` names unchanged.
