# Proposed Garden Universal Free-Use Policy

**INACTIVE DRAFT — NO PRESENT LICENCE, RIGHTS WAIVER, OR PATENT ABANDONMENT.**

This document records proposed terms only. Publication on a work branch or in a draft pull request does not enact them. Existing effective terms remain unchanged.

## Proposed foundational rule

Garden ideas are free knowledge for everyone, everywhere, always.

Anyone may use, study, implement, modify, combine, distribute, and commercialize covered Garden ideas and material for any purpose, inside or outside Garden, without licence fees, royalties, compulsory design payments, or charges for that use payable to Ankit Agrawal or any Garden licensing body.

The proposed permission would be worldwide, perpetual, and irrevocable once validly adopted. It would not depend on nationality, membership, Garden implementation, political affiliation, or a payment to the originator. Any voluntary appreciation or design payment would be optional and would not condition use.

For rights he controls, the originator proposes not asserting copyright, patent, or other IP restrictions to block covered use or demand payment. This is a proposed permission/nonassertion policy, not a declaration that a patent has been abandoned or that all intellectual-property rights have ceased to exist.

Use would be at each user's own responsibility, without a warranty of correctness, suitability, safety, or certification. Free use would not authorize unlawful conduct or confer governance authority.

## Scope and reconciliation required before activation

Covered scope proposed for review: the originator-controlled Garden design ideas, specifications, documentation, and implementations in the Garden repositories. Third-party code, data, models, contributions, rights, and licences cannot be waived by this document.

No money, common-fund assets, authorship, ownership registers, or other people's rights would be transferred. Separate taxes or financial obligations unrelated to permission to use Garden are outside this proposed licence policy.

Activation would require explicit reconciliation of operative licences and patent/use notices across repositories. Historical commits and immutable published design versions would remain provenance records rather than being rewritten.

**This draft is not an effective grant.**
