# v15.11 Candidate Delta — Cross-Model Capability Measurement

Status: CANDIDATE — protocol review pending; no canonical admission.

## Proposed addition
Add a recurring evidence process that compares available model families on fresh, blinded, reasoning-focused tasks using the frozen Model Capability Comparison Process. The process is provider-neutral, preserves exact identities/settings/raw evidence, distinguishes deterministic verification from model adjudication, preserves disputes, controls contamination/home-field effects, and reports uncertainty rather than a universal intelligence score.

## Trigger/cadence
Comparison may run periodically or on a material model/capability event, but cadence alone never changes Garden authority or truth. Each epoch uses fresh/controlled items and auditable receipts.

## Outputs
Pairwise distinguishability, category profiles, cheap-vs-frontier gap, accuracy-cost Pareto frontier, longitudinal change, author/question quality, disputes and evidence strength.

## Non-effects
A benchmark winner gains no authority, Proof, canonical status, permission, admission power, or automatic reviewer slot. Model-quality evidence may inform later governed selection only through the separately applicable selection/review process.

## Admission condition
Do not promote this delta until the protocol itself has completed independent multi-family adversarial review, reconciliation, frozen v1.0, executable verification tests and at least one auditable pilot epoch.
