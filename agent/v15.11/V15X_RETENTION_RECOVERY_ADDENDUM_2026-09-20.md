# Garden v15.11 — 15.x Retention Recovery Addendum

Status: NONCANONICAL WORKING-CANDIDATE SOURCE RECOVERY
Date: 2026-09-20
Authority effect: NONE

## Why this addendum exists

A direct comparison of the retained v15.7 complete working design against the compact v15.10/v15.11 text projections found that several named 15.x families were not explicitly represented in the compact current Catalogue projection. A blanket "nothing lost from 15.x" claim is therefore not justified by the compact projection alone.

This addendum restores those identified semantic families as retained current candidate material. It does not claim implementation, proof, certification, deployment or canonical admission.

## Recovered v15.x families

### Operational Closure Foundation (OCF)
Retain OCF as the closure discipline for whole-design work. Closure manifests bind source root, DesignEpoch, scope, requirements, inherited obligations, discovered gaps, owner map, dependency/evidence roots, coverage state, unresolved frontier and the exact scoped closure claim. Gap records retain semantic, algorithmic, data, empirical, composition, runtime, resource, safety, security, privacy, authority, legal, validation and deployment gaps. Partial coverage or resource exhaustion cannot be relabeled COMPLETE.

### Rights and Restrictions Context (RRCTX)
Retain RRCTX for jurisdiction-, licensing-, export-control-, secrecy-, disclosure-, localization- and similar context. RRCTX contextualizes applicable constraints but does not invent universal substantive law or create authority. Missing or unresolved jurisdictional qualification remains non-PASS.

### AI Development / Generation (AI-DEV)
Retain the AI-DEV family governing generated implementation, compilation, elision and machine-produced development artifacts. Generated code or design output does not self-certify, self-authorize or silently elide protected semantics. Independent owner/checker boundaries remain applicable.

### Compliance Standards Mapping (STD / COMPLIANCE-STD)
Retain source-authenticated, versioned, jurisdiction- and scope-bound mapping from external normative instruments to requirement instances, Garden bindings and conformance assessment. Assessment, admissibility and external certification remain distinct. An external certification exists only through the authenticated act of the applicable external authority.

### Engineering Design / Interoperability (ENG-DES)
Retain engineering-design and interoperability contracts, including explicit assumptions, units, interfaces, operating envelopes, failure behavior, verification obligations and cross-system compatibility. Similar interfaces or representations do not imply physical or semantic equivalence.

### Assurance Hardening
Retain the v15.x assurance-hardening families including degradation, hardware/software assurance ceiling, common-cause/correlation concerns, physical baselines, qualified component/configuration information, control-flow/integrity, security references, invariant deltas, driver diversity, epistemic workflow, novelty/safety hardening, legal-form and observability artifacts, resilience/fault injection, provenance extension, model audit and cryptographic-erasure distinctions.

Key retained rules include:
- rollback of model state, authority and external-world effects are distinct;
- safety-critical novelty reduces autonomous authority before promotion;
- unmodelled risk requires a justified risk envelope rather than invented precision;
- value/intent interpretation needs semantic validation, not merely hashes;
- introspection stays within declared resource/side-effect bounds;
- degradation follows a pre-authorized plan and cannot shed Constitution/HSA/K0P protections;
- material cross-modal conflict reduces actuation authority until resolved or safely bounded;
- adaptation remains inside declared scope and requires promotion to escape it;
- assurance claims cannot exceed the evidenced complete hardware/software stack;
- fallback targets a context-defined minimum-risk condition rather than assuming universal halt.

### Event-driven routing and constitutional-event coherence
Retain typed situation/applicability/routing artifacts and explicit UNKNOWN/AMBIGUOUS/INCOMPLETE outcomes. Routing artifacts do not create authority or proof merely by existing. Constitutional events do not themselves authorize containment. Executed containment/restriction requires its own valid typed authorization/admission path. Contradictory event tuples are rejected; PASS cannot coexist with a violation classification.

### Process triggers, subscriptions, scheduling and continuous work
Retain event-driven trigger/subscription/scheduling semantics. A trigger is an applicability signal, not authority. Repeated/continuous work remains bounded by ownership, resource, termination, safety, privacy and authority rules.

### Belief timeline, design mirror, future stress, global consistency and failure learning
Retain temporal belief/evidence state, design-mirror comparison, future-stress testing, global consistency checking, ontological debt, historical summaries and failure-learning semantics. Historical learning creates new evidence/counterfactual branches; it does not rewrite accepted history.

### Presentation, LSP and lifecycle/deprecation
Retain presentation/document-ownership rules, language-server/tooling profile semantics, and deprecation/sunset lifecycle. Removal cannot discard mandatory dependency, safety, rights, privacy, legal, retention, reconstruction or migration obligations. Historical identity/provenance required for audit remains retained subject to privacy rules.

### Algebra formalization details
Retain explicit operator laws and non-equivalences across Process, Policy, Decision, Conformance and Evidence algebras. UNKNOWN/STALE/BLOCKED/INCONCLUSIVE/TIMEOUT/RESOURCE_UNKNOWN remain explicit through composition. Stochastic operators declare randomness/budget semantics. Cross-algebra bridges preserve scope, provenance, uncertainty and authority. Hard rights/safety/authority constraints are not ordinary weighted preferences. Pareto selection does not silently choose among incomparable candidates.

### CPI semantic-preservation rules
Retain CPI rules including:
- maintenance preserves material exceptions;
- proof, likelihood, source trust, uncertainty and preference remain distinct;
- bounded uncertainty returns partial/UNKNOWN rather than invented proof;
- bridges preserve claim status, units, time, scope and effects or report loss;
- model-generated explanation cannot substitute for the selected checker;
- prediction/simulation is not observation;
- material drift invalidates dependent assurances;
- consequential sensor conclusions retain measurement provenance and interpretation uncertainty;
- learning/exploration actions receive the same physical/authority constraints;
- expected reward or information gain cannot bypass hard permission/privacy/safety constraints.

### AGI-specialization exact-family retention
Retain the named TED, SEA, MAC, NRS, VIP, SHE, EC, CSR, AR and MI families with their inherited hard constraints and test/invariant families. Their compact names are not permission to discard their detailed requirements.

### Bootstrap/Garden fleet distinction and model routing
Retain the distinction between an external bootstrap fleet and an internal authorized Garden fleet. Model selection is capability routing, not authority creation. Nominal model plurality does not establish independence where control lineage is shared.

### Epistemic debt and improvement frontier
Retain explicit epistemic-debt tracking and bounded improvement cycles. DO_NOTHING remains a first-class comparator. Hard constraints are checked before optimization; a proposal is not accepted merely because it dominates another proposal.

## Retention rule

Where this addendum conflicts with a compact omission in the v15.10/v15.11 review projections, the omission is treated as a representation defect, not intentional semantic deletion. Exact older identifiers, schemas, invariants and tests remain recoverable through the retained predecessor corpus until migrated into the current Catalogue with explicit equivalence/disposition.

No family above creates new authority by being recovered.

## Remaining closure

This addendum repairs identified semantic omission at the source-design level. It does not prove exhaustive byte-level retention. Exact standalone materialization remains separately blocked until the bound large source bytes are durably available or a governed replacement package supersedes those unavailable byte identities.
