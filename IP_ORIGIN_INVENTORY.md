- [`IP_ORIGIN_SUPPLEMENT.json`](IP_ORIGIN_SUPPLEMENT.json) — 31 additional archive-recovered/versioned historical records plus copyright, trademark and trade-secret candidate categories.

## Core rule

Garden-origin means the available provenance supports Garden as the source of a specific mechanism, combination, implementation or expression. It does not convert known science, mathematics, standards, generic algorithms, public prior art, or third-party technology into Garden property.

Historical TRAIN patent numbering changed repeatedly. Bare identifiers such as `F14`, `F16` or `F17` therefore have no stable meaning unless their dated snapshot is included. The supplement preserves conflicting snapshot-specific meanings rather than silently rewriting history.

## Protection-route screening

Each candidate is reviewed separately for plausible routes such as patent candidate, copyright expression, trademark candidate, trade-secret candidate, design right, contract/licence, or abstract idea with no automatic exclusivity. Copyright/trademark/trade-secret candidate lists are in the supplement.

The public automated audit uses the repository's bounded OpenRouter IP-origin review lane. The current approved reviewer families are DeepSeek, Qwen, GLM and Mistral. Specialist roles rotate across families and every family cross-examines each batch. Model outputs are evidence only.

## Ongoing maintenance

The Garden Hourly Builder and Daily Candidate workflows now require new or materially changed Garden mechanisms to be compared against this register and historical lineage. Public inventory changes must pass the bounded multi-family IP-origin review before their screening classification is treated as reviewed.
