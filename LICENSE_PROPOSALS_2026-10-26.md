## Proposal P-2026-10-26-A — Worldwide use and Garden Common Fund

**Status:** Proposed for review; not approved.

Proposed terms:

1. **Worldwide use and 99% contribution.** Garden ideas may be used worldwide. 99% of economic benefits attributable to using Garden ideas must be paid into the Garden Common Fund. The calculation must be transparent and must not count the same benefit twice.
2. **Reduction to 50% only with owner approval.** The rate remains 99% unless the repository owner expressly approves reducing it to 50%. No other condition or person can authorize this reduction.
3. **Fund investment and spending.** All money collected for the Garden Common Fund must be placed in fixed deposits targeting a 6% annual return. The 6% figure is a target, not a guarantee. During the repository owner's lifetime, Fund money may not be spent, transferred out for expenditure, or disbursed without the owner's prior express permission.
4. **Decisions after the owner's death.** After the repository owner's death, a randomly selected group of at least 100,000 verified, distinct people worldwide shall decide the Garden Common Fund's governance and any proposed spending or disbursement through a publicly auditable process that protects participants' personal information.

### Review questions

Before any adoption, reviewers should examine definitions and calculation of attributable economic benefits; auditability and dispute resolution; fund custody, jurisdiction, taxes, and investment risks; the practical feasibility of fixed deposits and the 100,000-person selection process; participant verification and privacy; and consistency with the existing canonical terms. Listing these questions does not itself approve or reject the proposal.
